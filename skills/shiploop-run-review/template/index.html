The contract behind every row: the script owns the flow. The skill calls it, does exactly the step its packet names and calls back; the state is the only source of truth.

3. Findings and options

Each finding says what we expected, what we saw and what Claude advises; under it, the options that would put it right. Tick any mix. Your ticks stay in this browser, and step 4 turns them into one prompt.

Where strip: one cell per visit in order. Outlined cells are the stage the finding belongs to; amber is revise or replan, red blocked, dashed or hatched skipped. A letter under a cell names its outcome.
