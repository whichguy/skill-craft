$skill-craft:shiploop

Build and deliver a finished, working Salesforce chat application that feels like seamless, elegant Grok-style chat. I want to open it, ask for help in ordinary language, and get useful human answers that make my work easier. Run the assistant's work natively in Apex, with Grok/xAI providing inference.

I want the conversation to be the center of the experience: polished typography, comfortable reading, clear composition, and replies that explain what matters without overwhelming me. When I ask a follow-up, the bot should understand the conversation, ask a useful clarifying question when needed, and help me reach an answer or finish a task. I want a helpful assistant I can work with naturally.

I want to name my bots and keep multiple saved conversations, returning to the right bot and discussion without losing my place. I should be able to save reusable instructions and tasks, use them again, and change them when my needs change. I want to inspect what a bot remembers about me, correct mistakes, and ask it to forget something. My information should stay separate from another user's, and each bot's memory should stay separate from my other bots.

Within the permitted synthetic Salesforce records, I want to ask real questions, receive accurate answers grounded in records I am allowed to read, and follow up naturally. When I ask to create or update something, I want to see the exact proposed changes before deciding. Nothing should be written until I explicitly confirm that proposal. Rejecting or cancelling it should leave the records alone. Repeating an action or reloading at an awkward moment should not accidentally apply it twice, and I should understand what actually happened.

For longer tasks, I want useful work to continue while the app is closed. When I return, I want an honest account of what finished, what is still happening, and anything that needs my attention. I want to steer ongoing work with a follow-up or cancel it, with a clear explanation of what can still be stopped and what has already happened. If something fails or is interrupted, help me recover without guessing or losing the conversation.

Help me get started through guided, recoverable setup that explains what I need without exposing secrets. Make everyday use comfortable on a desktop, in a narrow window, and with the keyboard. Deliver the complete working experience so I can use it confidently.
