A research round (journal entry R8 in docs/shiploop-parallel-execution-journal.md, reports in docs/experiments/parallel-plan-execution-20261114/plan-dependencies-20261115/) found: no shipped harness provides a merge queue; the two published studies that measured wall-clock (CAID, Li et al.) found no gain; conflicts that matter are semantic ones no plan shows.
