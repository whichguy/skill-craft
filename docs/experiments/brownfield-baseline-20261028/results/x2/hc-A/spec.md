### R-4 Ping a check by UUID

- Any HTTP method is accepted without a CSRF token; HEAD, GET and POST are the documented ones (templates/docs/http_api.md:13, hc/api/tests/test_ping.py `test_post_works`, `test_head_works`)
- A success ping sets the check up (from new, down or paused), a fail ping sets it down and records a status change with reason "fail", a start ping marks a run as started without changing last-ping time or status (a paused check stays paused), and a log ping is only recorded, with no state change and no status change record (hc/api/models.py `Check.ping`, hc/api/tests/test_ping.py `test_it_changes_status_of_paused_check`, `test_start_does_not_change_status_of_paused_check`, `test_log_endpoint_works`)

- An unknown UUID is answered 404 with body "not found" (hc/api/tests/test_ping.py `test_it_handles_missing_check`)
- A success response is 200 with body "OK", carries `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and, when a body limit is configured, `Ping-Body-Limit: <n>`, and is marked uncacheable (hc/api/views.py `ping`, hc/api/tests/test_ping.py `test_it_works`, `test_it_never_caches`)

- The Pinging API document promises that pinging a check more than 5 times per minute may be rate limited; this repository applies no per-check or per-client rate limit to pings (templates/docs/http_api.md:53, hc/api/views.py `ping`)

- A check has one of three schedule kinds: simple (an expected period between pings), cron (a five-field cron expression evaluated in a chosen time zone) or OnCalendar (one or more systemd-style calendar expressions, one per line, evaluated in a chosen time zone and matching when any line matches) (README.md:36, README.md:42, templates/docs/configuring_checks.md:33, hc/api/models.py `CHECK_KINDS`)
- Every schedule has a grace time: how long to wait after the expected time before declaring the check down; the defaults for a new check are a period of 1 day and a grace of 1 hour, and both must be between 60 seconds and 31536000 seconds (one year) (README.md:36, hc/api/models.py `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT`, `DEFAULT_GRACE`, hc/front/forms.py `AddCheckForm`, hc/api/views.py `Spec`)
- The next expected ping is the last ping plus the period for simple checks, and the first schedule match after the last ping for cron and OnCalendar checks, computed in the check's time zone and correct across daylight-saving transitions; an OnCalendar schedule with no further matches means the check stays up indefinitely (hc/api/models.py `Check.get_grace_start`, hc/api/tests/test_check_model.py `test_status_works_with_timezone`, `test_get_status_handles_autumn_dst_transition`, `test_status_handles_stopiteration`)
