Write slug.py, a small command-line tool: `python3 slug.py TITLE` prints TITLE as a URL slug on standard output and exits 0. Lowercase the letters, turn each run of spaces or punctuation into a single hyphen, and drop hyphens from both ends, so "Hello, World!" gives hello-world and "  Many   spaces here " gives many-spaces-here. Titles with letters outside A to Z, such as é or ü, are my call and not yours: the SEO team has not decided whether those letters are changed to their plain letter or kept as they are, and I will tell you when they have, so do not choose. Until then, print "undecided: non-ASCII" on standard error and exit with status 2 for such a title. Add unit tests with unittest.
