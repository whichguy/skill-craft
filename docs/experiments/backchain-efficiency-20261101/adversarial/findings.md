# Adversarial review: Backchain 2026-11: shipped toolkit, scripted Until Loop binding, native card, first-trivial gate, lean synthesis prompts

Reviewer: codex:gpt-6.1-sol@medium

- **F1 (high, regression)** finish can certify convergence from an unrelated or fabricated terminal receipt; it checks only status == complete, without binding the receipt to this contract, candidate, reports, or selected runtime.
  - Experiment (scenario): Freeze a converge run, record one unchanged review, and place a complete receipt from another run at its receipt path. Run finish. Refuted if: finish refuses convergence because the receipt belongs to another binding and does not establish this run's required reviews.
- **F2 (high, regression)** The supposedly frozen request, caller bounds, baseline, contract, and runtime resources can change without invalidating verify or finish.
  - Experiment (scenario): After freezing and recording a valid cycle, separately mutate the frozen request, caller packet, baseline, contract, and selected runtime; invoke verify after each mutation. Refuted if: Every mutation is rejected as a changed binding before convergence can be reported.
- **F3 (high, overreach)** Source-aware edits outside caller bounds can pass the toolkit's claimed obligation checks, including removal of protected work.
  - Experiment (scenario): Supply bounds protecting an existing step and forbidding new edges. Remove that step or add a forbidden edge while keeping the graph structurally valid; submit an assessment with empty findings. Refuted if: record refuses the edit and identifies the violated bound; it cannot be accepted merely by reporting a difference.
- **F4 (high, untested-case)** The toolkit can record a stale or empty assessment as a qualifying trivial review without establishing that the reviewer inspected the candidate or performed the required lens and source checks.
  - Experiment (scenario): Submit an unchanged candidate with change_class=trivial, empty checks/findings/gaps/blockers, and a missing or stale candidate_identity; request a satisfied exit. Refuted if: record rejects the assessment until candidate identity and required review evidence are supplied.
- **F5 (high, regression)** Source-aware callers receive the wrong result contract and can lose the final source assessment.
  - Experiment (scenario): Complete a source-aware run through finish and pass result.json directly to a consumer enforcing the documented source-aware response shape. Refuted if: The consumer receives the required review object, final source disposition, and review.convergence without a host inventing an undocumented transformation.
- **F6 (medium, measurement)** Dropping the confirming review is supported by too little and too correlated evidence to establish that it preserves defect detection across standalone uses.
  - Experiment (arm): On blind held-out cases, continue runs after their first trivial review with an independently contextualized confirming reviewer. Blindly adjudicate any additional findings against the request. Refuted if: A prespecified adequately powered sample bounds additional material defect detection below an agreed acceptable rate, with no consequential regression from stopping early.
- **F7 (high, measurement)** The candidate adopts lean synthesis before the owner's required quality cohort, and the old study does not establish the combined candidate's behavior.
  - Experiment (arm): Run the specified cohort against the actual 0.6.6 control and the exact combined candidate, retaining all attempts and adjudicating semantic and workflow outcomes separately. Refuted if: The candidate meets a prespecified quality-first adoption threshold overall and in the previously losing families before token or time savings determine adoption.
- **F8 (medium, overreach)** The generated contract forbids evidence gathering that some supported planning tasks require, potentially turning recoverable source questions into blocked runs.
  - Experiment (scenario): Use a source-aware request explicitly authorizing a read-only fetch of a normative source whose current revision is necessary to resolve a planning gap. Refuted if: The run obtains the authorized evidence and completes without violating its frozen authority or requiring a replacement binding solely because the toolkit imposed a blanket prohibition.
