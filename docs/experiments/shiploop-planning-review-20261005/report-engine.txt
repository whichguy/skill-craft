5. ANSWER (3): STATE, VALIDATION AND SAVED RUNS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

New state: one key, `planning_review`. No other field. The covered stages are derived from the closer through `phase_covering`, and `active_improve` and `improve_results` keep their shape.

Validation (prototype, scratch experiment E3):
- `state["planning_review"]` must be one of the three values.
- Required records are history entries whose stage is in `reviewed_stages(mode)`. Under `phase`, a step-plan counts only when its accepted result is a no-test result.
- Allowed record stages come from `_improve_stages(state)`. A record at a stage the mode never reviews is refused.
- An active child must pass `_improve_checkpoint(mode)`.
- Results: dropping a required record is refused in phase (plan, test-spec, system-test-author) and in none (system-test-author, release-plan). A forged record at spec is refused in both.

Saved runs, one supported version:
- A run recorded by 1.21.x (no key) is refused with the existing generic missing-fields message and `FRESH_RUN_HINT`. [M: scratch experiment E4/E8, run through the real CLI `next`.]
  - Message: "navigator state has unsupported or missing fields (missing: planning_review) ... Preserve it; this ShipLoop cannot resume it. Start new work with init or workspace start in a fresh --run-dir."
- A retry naming another value is refused, naming the recorded value and the fresh-run route.
- An invalid value is refused by argparse.
- No migration, no `--set`.
- Operational consequence: any in-flight E2E run saved by 1.21.x, including `--resume-run` runs, is refused after the release lands. The same happened with `backchain_passes`.

6. GAPS IN THE CANDIDATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

a) The exit criteria of covered stages vanish unless `stage_exit` changes. [M: E9, exit clause check]
   - At a phase plan child the frozen exit condition contains plan's done-when criteria but NOT spec's or test-strategy's.
   - This is the exact place where "steps iterate until a stated check confirms each exit criterion" would be lost.
   - Fix: `loop_contract.stage_exit` takes the covered stages. Extend the existing S-10 assertion in `test_improve_start_freezes_the_contract_and_starts_the_runtime` to every covered stage.

b) Child scope can be narrowed silently. Fix as in section 4 (script-owned candidate paths).

c) A no-test work item would get no step-plan review. [M: E5]
   - After step-plan the script records test-spec as not applicable for documentation-only items (`_record_not_applicable_tests`, SKILL.md "Test stages not applicable"), so no producer exists to carry the closing child.
   - Fix: under `phase` a step-plan child runs only when its done result is a no-test result (`item_scope.no_test_result(result)`); the validator mirrors it.
   - My prototype also parks a child for blocked or repeat step-plan results. I recommend the simpler rule instead: non-done results advance directly, as at spec and test-strategy.

d) Structural step-plan defects found at the test-spec child cannot be patched in place. [M: E6]
   - Fields `test_commands`, `paths`, `criteria`, `steps` exist only in the step-plan result. A test-spec child's `final_result` is a test-spec result.
   - The route is outcome `revise`, which the engine accepts at test-spec (`stage_spec._finish` adds it from test-spec through integration-verify). It returns to step-plan, counts against `MAX_REVISES = 2`, and the redone step-plan is accepted without a child.
   - The child packet text does not name `revise` as a final_result outcome today. It says done, repeat, blocked, or replan at outer stages only. It must be named.
   - Cost: a patch becomes a re-authoring pass. In the Luna redo the step-plan stage took 120.1 min with its loops against 38.7 min first time. Cheaper because there is no step-plan child on redo, but unmeasured.
   - Decision for the owner:
     - J1 (candidate, my recommendation): the child sees both documents and cross-document consistency.
     - J2: put the per-item child on step-plan and leave test-spec unreviewed. Structural fixes then patch in place, but test-spec is unreviewed.
   - The W1 defect that cost the most was a step-plan and test-spec defect that review missed under either arrangement (n=2).

Packet text under `phase` and `none` also needs (section 2.B):
- the union of stage references,
- the composed Improve prompt,
- the mode-aware sentences,
- the `revise` route sentence.

7. THE OWNER'S RULES, WHERE THEY STRAIN, AND WHICH SCRIPT-RUN CHECK CARRIES THE EXIT CRITERION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- **S-10 under `phase`.** It is not relaxed. Each child that runs keeps its no-cap exit rule and a frozen script-written contract. `phase` changes only what one loop's candidate is. The condition is gap (a): `stage_exit` must carry every covered stage's criteria.
- **S-10 and S-9 under `none`.**
  - S-10 does not require every stage to have a loop, but the owner's primary rule ("steps iterate until a stated check confirms each exit criterion") is directly strained for five stages. The carve-out must say so.
  - S-9 is not weakened as worded, because today's per-stage review exit is a model declaration. `stale_check_note` in `standalone.complete` says so: the import "does not prove ... review or check claims". The removed evidence was never script-run.
  - No script check proves the semantic done-when criteria (every request outcome maps to an acceptance criterion; independent oracle with RED and GREEN definitions).
- **Script-run checks that remain in every mode** [M: `dispatch` complete branch and the named modules]:
  - `_check_submitted_assumptions` (plan).
  - `_check_submitted_test_commands` and `item_scope.normalise_paths` (step-plan).
  - `_check_submitted_recorded_commands` (system-test-author, release-plan).
  - `_check_submitted_evidence`.
  - `_knowledge_gate`: required files non-empty at prepare, test-spec, release-plan, release-verify; credential screen; requirement IDs not dropped.
  - Structural `backchain-check` (record-only).
  - `no_test_item` path classification.
  - `_planning_sources_current`.
  - Later: the `test-red` gate (focused commands must fail inside a test), `test-green`, `regression`, `integration-verify`, `system-test`, `release-verify` reruns.
- **Where the gap is.** The first script-run check that can reject a bad step plan or test spec is `test-red`, after baseline and test-author. Both W1 revises came out of it: 165.9 min redo on Luna xhigh, 123 on 1.16.1 max. `none` leaves that as the backstop, with `MAX_REVISES = 2`.
- **Optional check, owner decision, not required for the option.**
  - A script-run requirement-ID traceability check at the test-strategy and test-spec closes: every R-id in `spec.md` appears in `test-strategy.md` and in the feature's `test-spec.md`. `knowledge_home._REQUIREMENT_ID` and `CLOSES` already provide the pieces.
  - It lifts the most mechanical review finding class (traceability, one of 5 warranted Luna findings) from model to script.
  - I could not read what the Luna traceability finding actually was, so whether this check would have caught it is [I].
- **"Enterprise rigour is standard."**
  - `phase` keeps a review of every covered stage and moves one commit's position.
  - `none` removes the model-run review and the experiments and reconcile route at planning.
  - It leaves quality, test, Backchain-one-pass, and the system-test-author, release-plan and end-of-work children in place.
- **SPEC admission.**
  - The anchor is the owner's 30-minute rule and the 2026-10-05 measurements, not an S-n clause. That matches the 2026-10-04 carve-out's form.
  - Amend the SPEC first, in its own commit: a dated carve-out beside S-10 and the "How the harness checks the clauses" row.
  - Candidate wording: option `planning_review`, values and default, "S-10 holds for every child that runs; `none` runs no review loop at spec, test-strategy, plan, step-plan, test-spec; those stages rely on the script-run checks listed here and the later RED and GREEN gates; the system-test-author, release-plan and end-of-work children, and the quality and test loops, are not covered".
  - Add the owner's 30-minute planning ceiling as a harness-recorded measurement. The ceiling is a trigger to rethink planning, not a verdict.

| | Luna xhigh (window 375.9) | Grok medium (window 72.7) |
|---|---|---|
| `none`, gross | up to 203.4 min, the whole child time [M]. Ledger L1: window about 172.5, net lower because pass 1 is partly a second authoring pass (74.4 min, share unmeasured) | up to 42.4, giving 30.3 [M arithmetic] |

- 12 of 22 Luna passes changed something (146.5 min) and 10 changed nothing (28.2 min). The cost is in finding passes, not confirming passes. L4 (one clean review instead of two) is worth only 8.1 min. A pass cap would violate S-10. `phase` and `none` are the levers that fit the rules.
