## 0. Summary

1. The candidate design is sound but needs refinements; none of them is blocking. The scratch prototype confirms the schedule logic is small: about 35 changed lines in `shiploop_prompts.py`, `shiploop_navigator.py` and `shiploop_protocol.py` give the three modes. [M, prototype]
2. The code treats seven results as "planning review" stages, not five. `PLANNING_REVIEW_STAGES = stage_spec.with_improve("always")` also contains `system-test-author` and `release-plan`. The option must be scoped to the five planning-phase stages through the stage table, or the OUTER reviews would be removed in `none`. [M]
3. Four design facts decide the shape of mode `once`:
   - Folding spec and test-strategy into the `plan` child is required, not optional. `improve-reconcile` and the plan-time experiments exist only on the plan child, and reconcile is legal only before `prepare`. [M `_reconciliation_permitted`, planning-experiments.md]
   - Folding `step-plan` into the `test-spec` child has a gap. The structured step-plan fields (`steps`, `test_commands`, `criteria`, `paths`) are script-consumed and live in the accepted step-plan record, which a test-spec child's `final_result` cannot amend. The only route is outcome `revise` (two per item, shared with later revises). I tested it. [M, prototype]
   - A covered stage's `done_when` reaches the review loop only if the script adds it to the child's frozen exit condition. This costs 564 B (plan child) or 461 B (test-spec child) of a 9,216 B contract budget. [M]
   - Test-spec is authored against an unreviewed step plan in `once`; today it is authored against a reviewed one. [M, graph order]
4. The 30-minute ceiling is not reached by the default. Bounds follow, all [I] except the removed-children totals:
   - Grok medium (window 72.7 min): `once` lands between 38.0 and 72.7 min. `none` lands at about 30.3 [M arithmetic, assuming no work shifts to the producers].
   - Luna xhigh (window 375.9 min): the ledger's own floor with no Improve and no Backchain loop is 115 to 131 min. `once` is estimated at about 198 to 346 min.
   - The owner's decision stands, but the owner should know this before choosing the default.
5. Every script-run check that exists at planning stages still runs in every mode, because they all sit at the producer's `complete` verb. [M] The only script-run check that caught the expensive W1 defect is the test-red gate. It fires five stages after the plan. [M]
6. The owner's own 2026-09-23 record lists "every planning result gets its own Improve child" as an invariant no phase may weaken, and 0.23.0 removed the cadence setting as "latest only". [M] This change reverses both. Option plumbing itself has precedent in `lint`, `delegation` and `backchain_passes`.

## 1. Findings

### F1. Where the schedule lives [M]

- `shiploop_stage_spec.py`: `Stage.improve` is `"always"` on spec, test-strategy, plan, step-plan, test-spec, system-test-author and release-plan, and `"last-item"` on carry-forward.
  - `with_improve(rule)` derives the sets. `guidance.PLANNING_REVIEW_STAGES = with_improve("always")` is the one set.
  - `STEP_PLANNING_STAGES` in the navigator is `PLANNING_REVIEW_STAGES` minus the prelude planning stages. It holds step-plan, test-spec, system-test-author and release-plan.
- `shiploop_navigator.py`, five places read the set:
  - `_improve_checkpoint(state, stage, result)` returns True for every result at those stages, including blocked and repeat.
  - `_validate_current_state` requires an `improve_results` record for every completed planning-stage action (`planning <= set(records)`, message "every planning-stage result"). It is the hard invariant that mode `once` and `none` must make mode-aware. A test-stage action recorded as not-applicable is excluded (`item_scope.no_test_item`).
  - `_improve_line(stage)` prints "Every {stage} result, including blocked and repeat, starts this action's Improve child." The delegation suite pins this string.
  - `_render_improve` selects `PLANNING_REVIEW_FOCUS` by the same set.
  - `apply` parks the action in `active_improve`, and `finish_improve` records `improve_results[action]`.
- Consumers tolerate a missing record. `shiploop_planning_context._improve_projection` returns `{}` for None, `context_index` and `_planning_source_lines` use `.get`, and the run-review exporter derives children from the `improve/<child>/` directories. [M]
- Independent oracles pin the schedule on purpose:
  - `shiploop_navigator_dry_run.REVIEWED3` and `activity()`.
  - `skills/shiploop-e2e-audit/harness/dag_replay._PLANNING_CHECKPOINTS`, `_path_steps` and `_literal_checkpoint`.
  - `workflow_review.PLANNING_REVIEW_STAGES`. It reports "accepted planning-stage result has no Improve record" as unknown and incomparable.

### F2. Constraints the code puts on folding [M]

| Constraint | Evidence | Consequence for `once` |
|---|---|---|
| Reconcile only before prepare | `_reconciliation_permitted` requires `stage == "plan"` and a bound plan child | The global fold must close at `plan`. A later fold would lose the restart route |
| Plan-time experiments only on the plan child | planning-experiments.md ("applies only to the initial bound Plan Improve child") | They survive in `once` and vanish in `none` |
| Structured step-plan fields are script-consumed | `_canonical_result`: `steps`, `criteria`, `paths`, `test_commands` only on step-plan; read by `test_loop._step_plan`, `implement_progress`, `item_scope` | A test-spec child cannot amend them. Defects go through `revise`, the budget is `stage_spec.MAX_REVISES = 2` per item, and exhaustion ends in "blocked, awaiting the user" |
| Spec and test-strategy records carry no structured fields | `_canonical_result` | No amend gap at the global fold. The files are edited in place and the closing result states changed decisions |
| Exit criteria reach a child through `loop_contract.stage_exit(child["stage"])` | `improve_start_contract` | A folded child must add `stage_exit` per covered stage, or the covered `done_when` is not in the frozen exit |
| Contract budget `CONTRACT_BUDGET` = 9,216 B | `shiploop_loop_contract` | See the measurements in F7 |
| Stage-keyed Improve prompt blocks | `_improve_prompt`: `plan_guard` (plan and step-plan), `coding_review` (step-plan), Backchain owner text, test-facility block | A folded child needs the union of its covered stages' blocks. The stage-specific extras are 0.6 to 2.6 KB |
| Test-spec is authored before step-plan is reviewed in `once` | graph order | The review's cascade risk. A step-plan `revise` also wastes the test-spec work |

Today, `references/execution-planning.md` says the plan is reviewed "before the script releases `test-spec`". That sentence becomes false in `once`.

### F3. What carries each planning exit criterion today, and what S-9 really has [M]

Every planning stage prints "Goal / Done when (confirm each before calling done; keep going until all hold)". The source is `_goal_lines`, from the stage table `done_when`. This is the owner's 2026-09-24 rule that the iterate-until-confirmed loop lives in the step prompt.

The script gates run at the producer's `complete` verb, before `apply`, in every mode. They are `_check_submitted_evidence`, `_check_submitted_assumptions`, `_check_submitted_test_commands`, `_check_submitted_recorded_commands`, `_knowledge_gate` and the `_canonical_result` shape rules.

| Stage | `done_when` | Script-run today (all modes) | Only a review covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| spec | outcomes map to criteria; criteria verifiable; NFRs assessed; unaffected requirements preserved | cited files exist; `knowledge.check` refuses a dropped committed R-ID and credential-like lines (when `docs/shiploop` changed) | completeness and verifiability of criteria, NFR assessment |
| test-strategy | every criterion maps to a check; harness selected; planned checks marked planned | cited files exist; knowledge screen | the mapping, harness choice |
| plan | every criterion owned; dependency order; assumption list complete | assumption list shape, open consumers in the queue, evidence files exist, probed entries cite non-empty output; `backchain-check` (structural, record-only, host-run) | ownership completeness, assumption completeness |
| step-plan | opening sentence; steps; deps; commands; paths | `_normalise_steps` (unique ids, deps on earlier steps), `_check_submitted_test_commands` (commands, criteria each named by a command, `paths`, command listing) | semantic quality of commands |
| test-spec | each criterion has a case; oracle plus RED/GREEN | knowledge close requires non-empty `plan.md` and `test-spec.md` | oracle independence, "a weaker stand-in fails it" |

The first script-run confirmation of the planning claims comes later:
- **test-red**: `shiploop_test_loop.judge(red=True)` returns "not-red" for "failed without any failing test: a syntax, import or setup error is not a meaningful RED". This is how a test spec that loads a module no step creates is found. It fires five stages after the plan (step-plan, test-spec, baseline, test-author, test-red).
- **Later gates**: test-green, regression, system-test.

An Improve child at a planning stage has never had content verified by script. `_derived_improve_receipt` needs `review-<n>.md` and `checks.md` to exist. `standalone.complete` checks the Until Loop terminal packet. `_unchanged_first_pass` is cross-checked against the Git snapshot. `_improve_change_gate` refuses uncommitted review edits, and the test commands are rerun only after a changing end-of-work review (carry-forward). [M] So what a planning child gives S-9 is loop mechanics the script counts, not content. Removing it removes a second look, not a script-run content check. The SPEC text below states this.

The contract and the packet both use it.
2. Contract: add `stage_exit` for each covered stage and the covered paths to the frozen scope. Do not rely on the model-written opening.
3. Improve prompt: take the union of stage-keyed blocks for the covered stages. Add one cross-check bullet to the focus when a child covers more than one stage: "a criterion with no check, or a test or check that needs a module, file or fixture no step creates". 6. Update the oracles per mode as literal tables: `REVIEWED3`/`activity()` in the dry-run, and `_PLANNING_CHECKPOINTS` and `workflow_review` in the audit harness.
7. In `none`, document the loss of reconcile and plan-time experiments, and that `--delegation ask-agent` plus `none` runs a parallel chain on an unreviewed graph.

It relaxes S-9 and the owner's rule that a step iterates until a check confirms each exit criterion, for the Improve review of planning results, and says so.) The run option `planning_review` (`--planning-review stage|once|none`, default `once`, recorded in `state.md` at `init` or `workspace start`, never changed afterwards) sets which of the five planning results `spec`, `test-strategy`, `plan`, `step-plan` and `test-spec` start an Improve child. `stage` is the earlier rule: each starts its own. `once` starts one child at the end of each planning phase: at `plan`, which also covers the accepted `spec` and `test-strategy` results, and at each work item's `test-spec`, which also covers that item's accepted `step-plan`; the other three advance without a child. `none` starts none of the five. `system-test-author`, `release-plan` and the last `carry-forward` keep their child in every mode. A child that starts is unchanged: a contract the script writes, the same two-review or unchanged-first-pass exit, no iteration cap. In `once` the script, not the model, lists the covered results in the child's scope and adds each covered stage's done-when criteria to its exit condition. What confirms the exit criteria of a result that advances without its own child, and in `once` before its late review, is what S-9 can name: the printed Done-when criteria and the producer's duty to confirm each before it reports done; ShipLoop's gates at `complete` (cited files exist; the plan's assumption list; the step plan's commands, criteria, paths and dependency order; the knowledge home's required files, credential screen and requirement-ID retention); the structural, record-only `backchain-check`; and the first script-run confirmation of a planning claim, the test-red gate, which runs the focused commands and refuses a failure that is not inside a test, then test-green, regression and system-test. No script-run check at planning, in any mode, covers whether spec criteria are complete and verifiable, whether the test strategy maps every criterion to a check, or whether test-spec oracles are independent and fail a weaker stand-in; S-9 holds for those only in the structural sense above. An Improve child's own S-9 evidence has only been loop mechanics the script counts (iterations, the review streak, a Git cross-check, the commit rule), never a check of the review's content, so `none` removes the second look, not a content check. In `once` that look comes after the documents that build on the covered results were written, and a covered result's accepted record cannot be amended: an item's step-plan defect that needs its steps, commands, criteria or paths changed is reported as `revise` (two per item, shared with later revises), and a changed decision in a covered spec or test strategy is made in the files and stated in the closing child's result.
