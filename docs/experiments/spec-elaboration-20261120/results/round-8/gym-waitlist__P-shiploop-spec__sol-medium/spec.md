# Class Booking App

## Scope and basis

On 2026-11-21 the user asked, under “The incoming request,” to “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” An eligible member can join a full class's waitlist, see and withdraw their own entry, and receive a confirmed booking when their turn comes and an eligible seat opens. Joining is consent to automatic booking under the app's existing booking terms. The member sees the outcome in the app.

The request establishes an existing class booking app and a full-class scenario. No prior spec, repository, discovery, research, selected baseline, product screen, or runtime was supplied. Actors and states below are a proposed contract. Preservation clauses protect the app's existing booking, eligibility, capacity, cancellation, and access rules where present. Their exact behavior and route are unobserved. There is no reference system for parity and no earlier requirements to reconcile. Unrelated account, schedule, and booking management stay within their existing scope. The waitlist adds no staff workflow, guest identity, contact data, or external delivery channel.

## Requirements

Acceptance lines use **Positive**, **Failure**, **Boundary**, **Surface**, and **Confirm**. Confirmation methods describe future verification.

### R-1 Join the waitlist

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “Add a waitlist … so full classes don't turn people away,” authorizes a path after a full result. Preserve normal booking when a place is available and existing booking eligibility. Add a waitlist action and explicit consent on the existing class booking surface. Class visibility and the booking contract are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A3 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — An eligible signed-in member viewing a full, bookable class occurrence can choose **Join waitlist**. Before committing, the action explains automatic booking and where to check the outcome. On commit the member sees **Waitlisted**, with no confirmed seat or change in booked capacity. **Failure** — A rejected join explains the actionable reason when the member is entitled to know it and produces no entry. **Boundary** — An occurrence is full for direct booking when all seats are booked or protected for the queue under R-4. Waitlist eligibility applies only while the occurrence is open and before its cutoff. **Surface** — Existing class booking view and member read-back. **Confirm** — Walk C1 for admission and eligibility, and C5 for cutoff. Compare capacity and membership before and after through the booking authority.
- **A2.** **Positive** — An eligible member can book through the existing path when there is a free seat and no waiting member with priority. **Failure** — If availability changed to full since the member chose **Book**, show the full result and offer **Join waitlist** as a separate explicit action. **Boundary** — If a fresh join reaches an open occurrence with an unclaimed seat and no active waitlist, create neither a booking nor an entry and present **Book**. **Surface** — Class booking view and authority. **Confirm** — C1, including retries after availability changes in each direction and authority read-back.
- **A3.** **Positive** — An active entry remains the member's sole entry when a fresh join repeats the intent, preserving its original priority. **Failure** — A member who already holds a booking receives the existing booking outcome and creates no waitlist entry. **Boundary** — The same member may independently wait for distinct occurrences if allowed by existing eligibility. **Surface** — Member's status, booking and waitlist state at the authority. **Confirm** — Repeated and simultaneous join checks in C1 and C6.

**State and data.** Join targets one existing member and one scheduled occurrence, including when a recurring class has other occurrences. The booking authority owns the durable active entry and its admission order. Joining changes no booking or capacity record. Active membership and a confirmed booking are mutually exclusive for the same pair. Operation recovery is in R-6.

**Domain rules and open gaps.** The queue is ordered by accepted joins per occurrence, including a definite order for simultaneous admissions. No priority tiers or waitlist-size product cap are assumed. The booking owner must supply the actual identity, eligibility, occurrence, capacity, and cutoff contracts before adapting the join or ordinary booking routes. A lower capacity than current enrollment, or a rule that requires fresh consent or charges at promotion, needs reconciliation with the product owner before exposing affected joins. See R-4.

### R-2 See one's own current outcome

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “so full classes don't turn people away,” implies a member can tell whether they are waiting or have a place. Preserve existing booking confirmation and class visibility. Add a private waitlist status on the class booking view. Current read surfaces and update behavior are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A3 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — On a current read, show **Waitlisted** for an active entry, the existing booking state when a booking exists, and the private ended outcome for a retained entry ended as ineligible while the occurrence is open. With no booking, show **Waitlist closed** for a visible occurrence that reached cutoff or was canceled. Otherwise show the ordinary class state with no active membership. **Failure** — On a failed read, show that current status is unavailable and offer retry. **Boundary** — Reload or reopening the app obtains the authoritative outcome for the signed-in member, including a promotion while the app was closed. **Surface** — Existing class view and, after promotion, the existing booking surface. **Confirm** — C1–C5 and C7 with a fresh read and an independent second session. Check the genuine booking and occurrence identity as well as visible text.
- **A2.** **Positive** — A sent action shows an accessible pending cue until its outcome is known. A success message follows a confirmed outcome. **Failure** — If the outcome is unknown, say so and offer status refresh or safe retry. A known rejection remains visible until resolved or dismissed. **Boundary** — An older response cannot replace a newer confirmed outcome, and a response for an old account or occurrence cannot update the current view. **Surface** — Waitlist controls, status text and accessible announcements. **Confirm** — Delay, fail, and reorder responses in C7 while observing the current control, status and announcement.
- **A3.** **Positive** — Current authorization governs reads and writes. **Failure** — A forbidden or cross-account request reveals no other member's identity, membership, priority, or booking and changes no protected state. **Boundary** — Discard protected display data when the current user signs out or switches account. Reject further exposure when authorization can no longer be confirmed, including during reconnection. **Surface** — Member UI and data returned to the client. **Confirm** — C7, comparing two authenticated views and inspecting returned data.

**State and data.** The client owns transient presentation, pending intent and request context for the current account and occurrence. Discard these on context exit. The authority owns waitlist and booking state. Only the member's own protected facts reach their client. Derived availability, as allowed by existing class visibility, is independent of private membership. Queue position and member lists are outside scope. In-app confirmation on read is the default. Background push, email, and SMS have no delivery commitment.

**Open gap.** The booking owner must identify the supported member view, booking entry point and authorization/revocation boundary before UI integration. The product owner would need to approve any required proactive contact or changes to existing notification obligations.

### R-3 Leave the waitlist

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “Add a waitlist,” implies members can withdraw a pending commitment. Preserve existing cancellation rules for confirmed bookings. Add **Leave waitlist** for an active entry. Current booking cancellation and authentication routes are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A2 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — The signed-in member can withdraw their active entry before cutoff and see that they are no longer waiting. The withdrawn entry is ineligible for promotion. **Failure** — A rejected withdrawal leaves state unchanged and explains the next action if known. Unknown outcomes use R-2 and R-6. **Boundary** — A repeated withdrawal of an already withdrawn entry has no additional effect. An explicit rejoin while full receives new priority. **Surface** — Class view and authority. **Confirm** — C2 with a fresh read, repeated withdrawal, then rejoin and release a seat.
- **A2.** **Positive** — When withdrawal is accepted first in a race with seat assignment, it rules out promotion of that entry. **Failure** — If promotion committed first, show the confirmed booking and direct the member to the existing booking controls. The waitlist action makes no booking cancellation. **Boundary** — A late retry for a withdrawn entry cannot withdraw a later rejoin. **Surface** — Class view, booking view and authority. **Confirm** — C6 with both orderings and an old retry after rejoin.

**State and data.** Target the member's particular enrollment attempt on that occurrence. Withdrawal removes active priority, leaves existing bookings untouched and never affects another entry. Recovery resolves the original intent against that entry. R-5 governs removal of terminal records.

**Open gap.** The existing booking cancellation eligibility and user-facing path require inspection before wiring the promotion-first outcome.

### R-4 Allocate freed seats in waitlist order

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “so full classes don't turn people away,” requires a path from waiting to a seat. FIFO automatic booking is a default inferred from the outcome. Preserve normal booking rules, occupancy limits, and existing authorized seat-release operations. Change which eligible claimant receives an authorized vacancy while a queue exists. Seat-release routes and promotion eligibility are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A3 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — Before cutoff, an effective booking cancellation that frees a seat triggers reevaluation by the booking authority with every member view closed. Allocate available seats to eligible active entries in accepted join order, one booking per member and occurrence. A promotion consumes its seat and ends its entry as one visible outcome. **Failure** — With no seat, the queue stays active. A rejected cancellation provides no vacancy and triggers no promotion from that request. **Boundary** — If several seats are available, process up to capacity or until the queue is exhausted. Any remaining valid seat returns to normal booking. **Surface** — Booking authority and member booking read-back. **Confirm** — C2/C3, observe both booking owners and the later normal booking path, including no-seat and denied cancellation controls.
- **A2.** **Positive** — Revalidate the head entry against current authorization and booking eligibility when assigning a seat. A definitively ineligible entry ends without a booking and yields to the next entry. Its member sees a durable outcome on their next authorized read, under the same visibility and retention rules. **Failure** — If eligibility or completion is unknown, protect the seat from bypass and retry or reconcile through the authority. Preserve queue order while unresolved. **Boundary** — A new direct booking cannot claim a seat owed to a waiting entry or reserved for reconciliation. Once reconciled, expose remaining unclaimed seats for normal booking. **Surface** — Authority, class view and private member status. **Confirm** — C4/C6 with definitive ineligibility, uncertain validation and a competing direct book.
- **A3.** **Positive** — A replayed or overlapping valid seat-release signal resolves against current occurrence state and fills only real vacancies. **Failure** — After interrupted allocation, the authority rediscovers and resolves pending vacancies without input from a member's view. **Boundary** — A stale signal cannot reopen a closed waitlist, exceed capacity, or assign two bookings for one entry. **Surface** — Authority and member booking read-back. **Confirm** — C5/C6 with interruption on both sides of commitment and duplicate or late signals.

**State and data.** The booking authority owns the affected occurrence, occupancy, booking identity, active order, and any incomplete assignment. A newly freed seat with an active queue is reserved for queue processing until the outcome is resolved. A promoted entry corresponds to a committed booking for that member and occurrence. A failed assignment never removes an eligible entry or misreports confirmation. Scope includes booking cancellations. Other existing changes that create available seats must honor the same priority if such routes exist.

**Domain rules and open gaps.** The product and booking owners must establish existing release routes, current eligibility checks, and whether a waitlisted member can be booked under existing payment, waiver, credit, schedule conflict and late-cancellation terms. If existing terms require new permission or an interactive step, the affected join and promotion path is unready pending a product decision. No new charges, offer windows, penalties or priority schemes are introduced by this spec. Planning must verify an existing way to coordinate eligible assignment across all seat-claim routes and resume pending work independent of the UI. The default keeps the seat protected on unknown outcome. Recovery deadlines and alerts are unresolved under Non-functional requirements.

### R-5 End the waitlist when booking closes

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “Add a waitlist … [to] full classes,” is scoped to bookable occurrences. Preserve existing class scheduling, cancellation, and confirmed-booking policy. Add closure and terminal cleanup for waitlist state. Existing lifecycle and retention rules are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A2 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — Close enrollment and promotion at the earlier of occurrence start or an existing earlier booking cutoff, or on class cancellation. End active entries without creating bookings and show closure for a class the member can still read. **Failure** — A late request or stale vacancy signal leaves the waitlist closed. **Boundary** — At the exact cutoff, closure wins over a join or assignment that has yet to commit. A booking committed before closure follows the existing booking lifecycle. **Surface** — Class status and booking authority. **Confirm** — C5 at one second before and exactly at the cutoff, plus a canceled-class variant.
- **A2.** **Positive** — Withdrawal, ineligibility, promotion and closure remove the entry from the active queue. Retain only what is needed for the member's outcome, replay safety and established retention obligations, then remove or de-identify expired waitlist data according to the applicable policy. **Failure** — An interrupted cleanup leaves terminal entries inactive and recoverable for cleanup. **Boundary** — Cleanup of waitlist data cannot cancel or hide a confirmed booking. **Surface** — Member view and authorized data/operations inspection. **Confirm** — Revalidate C2/C4/C5 after the applicable retention boundary and an interrupted cleanup using an authorized clock and inspection route.

**State and data.** The authority owns cutoff and lifecycle truth, current entries and any minimal terminal records. Active order is never carried to the next occurrence. No new personal fields. Retain the member and occurrence association only within access-controlled booking/waitlist data for the applicable lifecycle, and remove temporary presentation state as in R-2.

**Open gaps.** The booking owner must confirm the class lifecycle, cutoff clock/time zone convention, and whether canceling removes the view. The data owner must supply the applicable retention, account deletion, and replay-safety policy before cleanup behavior can be integrated or verified. Retention checks are open pending that policy.

### R-6 Recover safely from retries and interrupted changes

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, “Add a waitlist to our class booking app,” adds durable member intent to a workflow with competing seat claims. Preserve existing booking authority and permission checks. Add safe recovery for waitlist operations and their effect on booking. Current error, audit, and concurrency capabilities are unobserved. Implementation and checks A1–A3 are pending.

- **A1.** **Positive** — Retrying a pending action reconciles the original operation for the authorized member, occurrence, and intended entry. A committed join or withdrawal survives a lost acknowledgment. **Failure** — A definite rejection leaves the prior authoritative state intact. If state cannot be read, keep the outcome unknown and allow recheck. **Boundary** — A repeated operation has no second effect and an older outcome cannot supersede newer state. **Surface** — Authority and in-app status. **Confirm** — C6/C7 with lost acknowledgments before and after commit, repeated operations and a current read.
- **A2.** **Positive** — Concurrent joins, bookings, withdrawals, cancellations and closure resolve in a single authoritative order for that occurrence. **Failure** — Interrupted work leaves either the prior valid state, the committed result, or a protected vacancy awaiting reconciliation under R-4. **Boundary** — The final assignment guard applies current capacity, eligibility and cutoff even if earlier reads allowed the action. **Surface** — Booking authority and member read-back. **Confirm** — C5/C6 with controlled orderings, immediate read-back of each member's state, and checks for overbooking, double booking and bypass.
- **A3.** **Positive** — Authorized operations staff can retrieve app-owned outcomes for join, withdrawal, closure, promotion, authorization denial, assignment failure, and recovery using safe occurrence/operation correlation. **Failure** — Ordinary diagnostic delivery failure does not change the reported booking outcome. Any applicable mandatory audit gate must be established from existing policy. **Boundary** — Logs exclude names, contact details, credentials, and raw request payloads. **Surface** — Existing authorized operational surface and business read-back. **Confirm** — C6/C7 with outcome retrieval, redaction inspection, and diagnostic failure while checking business state.

**State and data.** Use the app's current booking authority for durable decisions and the current permission authority for member access. Match retries to an operation and its target entry. An operation identity identifies intent. Its acknowledgment can arrive later than the current entry or booking state. The booking authority can reconcile pending states after restart. Safe diagnostic correlations have existing restricted operator access and retention/purge rules. Client data never supplies ordering authority.

**Open gaps.** The booking and operations owners must identify the authority and current routes for coordination, durable completion, recovery, safe diagnostics, and any applicable audit gate. Verification needs an authorized way to order competing actions, stop an assignment, and read back state. These gaps affect the dependent integration and recovery checks.
