# Class Booking App Specification

## Source and scope

**Incoming request, 2026-11-21, “The incoming request”**

> Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.

The explicit outcome is an alternative to being turned away from a full class. The alternative must provide a real path to a booking when space opens. Membership in the queue does not guarantee a seat. Queue ordering, automatic promotion, and the interaction rules below are product defaults proposed for this run. The request names no reference product or parity target.

The request establishes that a class booking app exists and that classes can be full. No earlier specification, selected baseline, repository, discovery result, or research result was supplied. Existing booking eligibility, booking and cancellation terms, roles, occurrence lifecycle, all routes that alter capacity or bookings, and the supported consumer surface remain unobserved. Preservation below is an obligation to retain applicable existing behavior once recovered. Reconcile consequential differences through ShipLoop's spec correction route before dependent implementation. If already accepted when a contradiction is found, record the observation and its affected consumers, seek direction for a spec change, and route resulting corrective work through `add-work`.

**Vocabulary.** An occurrence is one scheduled instance of a class. Capacity and confirmed seat use apply to that occurrence. Full means confirmed seat use is at or above its authoritative capacity. A member is an authenticated person using the existing booking identity. A member is eligible to wait or be promoted if existing booking rules allow a booking for that occurrence, disregarding seat availability for the wait decision. Open means the occurrence admits new bookings under its ordinary lifecycle and the applicable booking cutoff has yet to arrive. The effective waitlist cutoff is the earlier of any ordinary booking cutoff and the scheduled start. The authority's time determines the boundary. Rank 1 is the first active entry. Status describes the member's current booking or waitlist outcome for that occurrence.

### Flows and transitions

- **F-1 Join or book (R-1, R-3, R-5).** Authorized member reads occurrence status, then submits Join when offered. Authority reevaluates at decision time and applies T-1 or T-6. If a seat is available after queue priority, the member can choose ordinary booking separately. Response/read-back resolves the outcome.
- **F-2 Check position or leave (R-2, R-5).** The owner reads current rank, selects Leave for the displayed entry and observes the outcome of T-2 or T-6. Retry confirms the original operation and current state.
- **F-3 Release and allocate (R-3, R-5).** Existing authorized booking or occurrence operation creates availability. The booking authority applies T-3 in queue order and resolves any competing ordinary booking against T-4. Member views reconcile through current reads even after disconnect.
- **F-4 End eligibility to wait (R-4, R-5).** The authoritative cutoff arrives or an authorized occurrence cancellation commits. T-5 governs subsequent actions and reads. The member sees the terminal reason when checking status.

| Transition | Before, trigger and guard | After, observable effect and recovery | Acceptance case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T-1 (F-1, R-1) | Open full occurrence. Eligible member with no booking or active entry submits current Join. | Append once to active order and return current own Waitlisted status. Confirmed seat use is unchanged. Active repeat preserves position; commit failure or uncertain reply uses T-6. | Join and isolate. Simultaneous entry. |
| T-2 (F-2, R-2) | Owner of active entry selects Leave on that entry before a competing transition terminates it. | End entry as Left and clear rank. Recompute remaining rank. Rejoin uses T-1 with a new intent. Repeat or superseded Leave uses T-6. | Leave, rejoin and recover |
| T-3 (F-3, R-3) | Availability exists in an open occurrence. Authority selects active head and evaluates current eligibility. | End definitively ineligible head as Ineligible and reconsider the same seat. For an eligible head, commit once as Promoted with a booking. Repeat until no allocatable seat or candidate remains. Uncertain eligibility or commit failure invokes protected recovery in R-3. | Promotion, competition and failure |
| T-4 (F-1, F-3, R-3) | Eligible member chooses normal booking while open and queue priority has been resolved. | With remaining capacity, apply existing booking rules. At zero capacity return full and offer Join when eligible. An unsettled allocation grants no competing seat. | Promotion, competition and failure |
| T-5 (F-4, R-4) | Existing cutoff or scheduled start, whichever comes first, or authorized cancellation. | All still-active entries become effectively terminal with the corresponding reason. No rank or admission remains. Physical cleanup can resume safely. | Cutoff and cancellation |
| T-6 (F-1 through F-4, R-1 through R-5) | Wrong actor or target, ineligible Join, available-capacity Join, duplicate or stale intent, authorization failure, interrupted action or read. | Reject, return current authorized status or reconcile per the acceptance line. No new business effect from an invalid or repeated intent. A stale response is discarded. A known failed commit preserves prior state. | Leave, rejoin and recover. Authorization and delayed reads. |

Booking state and waitlist state are distinct. An absent entry is the starting point for T-1. Active entries alone are ranked. Left, Promoted, Ineligible, Waitlist closed and Class canceled are terminal entry outcomes retained only for the applicable lifecycle. A promoted member's **Booked** display depends on the existence of a current confirmed booking. Later ordinary cancellation follows the app's cancellation status, with no implicit rejoin. Client loading, pending mutation, status unavailable, and confirmed display are transient view states. Occurrence closure and member authorization take precedence over stale view state.
