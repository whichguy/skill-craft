# Grok Bot for Salesforce

## Scope and evidence

The 2026-11-21 request is “Create a Grok bot in a Salesforce development org.” The consumer outcome is an authorized Salesforce user opening Grok Bot inside the designated development org and receiving a real Grok answer to a text question. The starting scope is an internal, interactive, single-turn assistant for synthetic and non-sensitive development prompts. CRM record access and actions, public channels, attachments, tools, and saved or shared conversations are outside that scope. This is a new product specification. There is no earlier accepted spec, discovery result, research handoff, or selected baseline. Existing org assets, settings, and access are unknown.

The provider documentation establishes the supported integration contract. The Salesforce documentation establishes supported platform mechanisms. The interactive workflow and lifecycle policies here are specification decisions. No target org, provider account, session, secret store, or runtime behavior was inspected. Grok integration is required, with no parity commitment to the Grok website or a separate Grok product.

The acceptance locators below are specification clauses. Each names an observation and a planned confirmation. Live checks are pending. If later evidence changes intent, behavior, or verification, revise the affected clauses and checks through ShipLoop's existing correction and add-work routes. Revalidate changed setup before relying on prior observations.

**State and domain rules.** Salesforce owns bot metadata, navigation, and authorization. The named org administrator or authorized delivery operator controls bot-specific metadata and configuration. Only the authorized access administrator assigns or removes effective grants. The deployment principal and runtime user may differ. Before any change, identify the org, read current metadata and effective assignments, and check for collisions. If they changed or the result of a write is uncertain, stop that change and reconcile by read-back before retrying. On retirement, withdraw access and remove bot-owned resources under the org administrator's authority. Neither installation nor runtime reads or writes CRM records, so record sharing, object/field authorization and data migration are inapplicable to this slice. Salesforce remains the authority for login and session validity.

**Open gaps.** The requester or Salesforce org owner must identify the exact non-production org and intended consumer, and resolve the authorized deployer, access administrator and allowed/denied test identities before the first dependent org write or check. The delivery operator must read back existing assets and permissions, confirm the target edition and navigation name, and obtain any separate grants needed for assignment or activation. No arbitrary default org is eligible. Deployment and access verification are unverified, owned by the delivery operator at release-check/release-verify. If the real consumer check requires a person, the designated consumer owns it at release-verify as an open item. Org-independent interaction and integration-contract work can proceed.

Hide raw errors and secrets. | A duplicate tap or key activation while pending creates zero extra submissions. No automatic retry. Check pending, error, recovery, request counts, and no success label for a mere acknowledgment. |

**State and domain rules.** The current draft is owned by the signed-in user's active view. A submit snapshots the valid prompt and creates one view-local pending operation. While pending, the composer is locked and Clear remains available. Success or failure ends pending and re-enables editing. Preserve the submitted question with its result or failure until the next valid send, Clear, or view end. The composer is empty after success and restores the submitted prompt on failure. Independent views have no shared draft or result. The configured model is the answer source. The bot does not promise answer correctness, live web knowledge, corporate knowledge, conversation memory or Salesforce actions.

**Service contract.** Salesforce is current authority for admitting an invocation. xAI is current authority for credential and model eligibility and the response to that invocation. Check current authorization on each new send and never serve a cached answer on rejection or outage. An admitted operation uses the binding at dispatch. Provider key revocation is reflected by the provider on subsequently rejected calls, and cannot retract text already displayed. A usable completed response for the active attempt is the success boundary. An acknowledgment, timeout or lost connection provides no proof of an answer or of non-processing. Consume the original response only, with no polling or background recovery promise. See R-4 for owned outcome evidence. n/a: no provider snapshots, incremental sync, event subscriptions, durable jobs or completion notifications are needed for a foreground text question.

**Open gaps.** The xAI team owner supplies the approved team, usable credential provisioning, permitted model, usage/billing readiness, and retention policy for R-3. The integration owner confirms the model's supported output controls, sets the model-specific timeout and input/output ceilings with headroom, and verifies target limits before dependent integration readiness. Team-specific throttles and any desired spend cap remain unresolved. Stage the generic paths and fixture-based checks independently. The integration owner at system-test and the designated consumer at release-verify own unverified live behavior on their respective surfaces. The team owner supplies person-only provider setup before the first live check.

### R-3 — Keep prompt state private and bounded

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request “Create a Grok bot in a Salesforce development org” supplies the basis for protecting prompts at the org-to-provider boundary. Preserve Salesforce's existing session and access controls and unrelated data. Add transient per-view state and a disclosed transfer to Grok. No prior retention contract is established. Provider documentation distinguishes retrievable response storage from audit retention. Actual team policy and org logging behavior remain unverified. Pending work includes lifecycle checks, sanitized diagnostics review and provider-policy confirmation. | Clause | Positive expectation | Failure expectation | Boundary expectation | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-3.a | Before Send the user sees that prompt text goes to xAI and is directed to use synthetic or non-sensitive text only. The provider receives the current prompt for generation with response retrieval storage disabled. | Credential configuration stays server-side. Browser traffic exposes neither the provider API key nor the provider authorization header or raw provider error. App-owned diagnostics exclude those fields and all prompt and answer text. | A prompt and answer may appear within the initiating view and traverse its authenticated request/response path. Org records and persistent browser storage hold no app transcript. Provider audit retention follows the actual approved team policy. | Inspect display, request/response payloads, application-owned diagnostic output and persistent stores during success and failure. Inspect the fixture provider request flags and obtain sanitized team policy evidence before live use. |
| R-3.b | **Clear** resets the question, answer, pending indicator and error in the current view. Reload, leaving the view, logout or account change starts a fresh empty view. | An obsolete completion or error cannot populate a cleared or replaced view or another user's browser. | Clear may abandon display of an in-flight request. It cannot unsend the request or delete provider audit copies. After Clear, a new send can proceed subject to the ordinary guards. | In the browser, hold one response, Clear, send a different prompt, and deliver the new response before the old one. Check both displays and request count. Reload, sign out and reopen as a second authorized user to confirm isolation. |
| R-3.c | A credential administrator can replace or remove the bot's credential using the org's supported secret-handling surface and the team owner's provider controls. | Missing, revoked or unusable configuration yields the safe unavailable or authorization failure and never reveals its value. | Only the named credential administrators handle key lifecycle. Removing the bot binding and disabling or deleting its dedicated key ends future use. No app copy or shadow credential persists. | Isolated development setup, runtime and provider controls. Confirm with sanitized status and behavioral read-back after a missing/revoked binding and after authorized restoration. |

**State and domain rules.** The active view holds at most one draft and one submitted question with associated pending state, answer or error. The view owns its theme choice and discards it at view end. Clear affects prompt state only. Salesforce processing holds the request and reply transiently for that operation. The application writes no prompt or answer to org records, persistent client storage or logs. Within a view, the current attempt wins, and callbacks for abandoned attempts have no effect. Authorization rests with current Salesforce grants, separate from any displayed answer. Provider processing is scoped to the approved team and key, and team policy governs access by the provider and retention/removal there. The provider documents a default 30-day encrypted audit window with automatic deletion. That window is conditional on the real team configuration. Clearing the screen has only local effect. Admin config survives across views until replaced or retired. Revalidate current configuration before an admin change, reconcile uncertain writes before retry, and verify new use after rotation. App-supported bounds and host quotas are in Non-functional requirements.

**Open gaps.** Before any real request, the xAI team owner confirms approved transfer, actual retention settings and compatible inference route, and the Salesforce admin confirms relevant org log/access policies for the live fixture. If policy disallows the proposed transfer or the confirmed retention cannot be met, keep live sending unavailable and revise the affected contract. No automatic detection or redaction of arbitrary personal data or secrets is promised. The admins own unverified setup evidence at release-check. The integration owner verifies live redaction and response storage behavior at system-test or the first authorized live verification surface.

## Submission

**summary** — Specified an internal Salesforce development-org bot that sends single-turn, text-only questions to xAI Grok and displays the reply in the same org. Assumptions are a Lightning Experience entry, assigned internal users, non-sensitive development inputs, an ephemeral question/answer view and a light-first design with a dark option. No material source conflict was identified. The exact org and authorized identities, xAI team/model and approved transfer/retention, operating bounds and org diagnostics remain open for the work that depends on them. Independent UI and contract work can proceed. Live checks are unverified and remain assigned above. At product-acceptance, at most one primary requirement may remain unverified and only with a passed lower-layer test for that requirement. Any required live observation remains due at its assigned boundary. This spec stage has no lower-layer result.
