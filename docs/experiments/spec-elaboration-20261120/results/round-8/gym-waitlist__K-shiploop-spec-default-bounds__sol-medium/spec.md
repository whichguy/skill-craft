# Class Booking App

## Scope and source

**Request, 2026-11-21, “The incoming request”** — “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.”

The request establishes that the app books classes and that a class can be full. Joining a queue is the direct outcome. Turning a vacancy into a booking for a waiting member is the intended benefit inferred from the request. Queue order, promotion, enrollment limits and feedback below are specification defaults. The standalone stage supplies no prior spec, discovery or research result, selected baseline, implementation, runtime or reference product. Existing behavior and platform capabilities are unverified. Preservation clauses are requirements for the change.

“Class” means a single scheduled occurrence, identified independently of its title or recurring series. “Member” means the authenticated principal who can act for their own booking under the app's current policy. An “eligible” member meets the applicable booking, membership and entitlement rules other than seat availability. “Open” means the occurrence is active, has yet to start, and is inside its existing booking window. The waitlist closes at the earlier of the existing booking cutoff (if any) and class start, or when the class is cancelled. A vacancy is usable capacity under the existing booking rules.

If an accepted vacancy is processed asynchronously, the booking authority owns enough durable state to reconcile it after interruption. **Domain rules and open gaps.** FIFO is per occurrence. There are no manual queue overrides, seat holds, acceptance timers, or priority tiers in this scope.
