# Grok for Salesforce

## Scope and basis

The incoming request on 2026-11-21 was “Create a Grok bot in a Salesforce development org.” The two outcomes are a bot that an authorized user can open in the designated Salesforce development org (R-1, C-01) and an actual Grok reply to a prompt submitted there (R-2, C-03). R-3 and R-4 define what users experience when that exchange fails, ends or crosses a trust boundary. The working assumption is a private, text-only chat for existing internal Lightning Experience users, with follow-up context within the open chat. The xAI API is the inference provider. No existing product baseline, parity target, discovery result or org observation was supplied. The absence of a baseline establishes no facts about the target org. The [public sources](#source-evidence) establish documented capabilities. The [architecture record](architecture.md) holds technology choices and their provisional parts.

### R-3 — Handle pending sends and failures

**Basis and delta.** “Create a Grok bot” (2026-11-21, incoming request) supplies the exchange. Add truthful pending status and recoverable failure behavior as a product assumption. Preserve earlier successful messages across recoverable failures. S2 and S4 describe the request boundaries. S5 establishes platform limits and provider error classes. No timeouts or failure events were observed in the actual target. Implementation and checks are pending.

| Case | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-07 | An eligible Send shows the pending user turn and **Waiting for Grok**, with the composer and Send unavailable until the attempt settles or New chat is selected. On completion, the committed pair replaces the pending turn. | Repeated Send while the attempt is pending is ignored. The app issues no automatic retry. | One active Send per open chat, including rapid click and keyboard activation of Send. Rate limit or transient service failure offers a later manual Send. An offline, disconnected or timed-out attempt reports that a reply could not be obtained. **State and data.** A pending attempt has one owner and one terminal disposition. Success, failure or invalidation removes its pending indicator. The first applicable terminal result wins for that attempt. If a timer and completion contend, a completion received before the agreed deadline may succeed if the chat is current. At or after the deadline the attempt fails. Errors never expose provider messages or secret-bearing diagnostics. A manual retry need not reverse an upstream effect from the earlier attempt. There is no durable job to resume.

**Open gap.** The implementer and product owner must settle the response and client deadlines with the target limits and an approved model. Deadline-specific verification remains open. There is no response-time guarantee, automatic backoff, offline queue or promise of provider-side cancellation.

## Non-functional requirements

- **Polished, accessible Lightning interface.** Design the transcript as the reading focus, keep the labeled composer and **Send** reachable, and use **New chat** and an accessible appearance control as secondary actions. Distinguish speakers by text as well as appearance. Use the **light Google Gemini** reference and **Material 3 Expressive** principles for layered tonal surfaces, readable grouped messages, a clear primary action and a distinct pending-turn treatment. The chat starts light with a dark option. Salesforce Lightning host conventions take precedence in the shell and established controls. Maintain a consistent spacing and type scale, semantic palette in both themes and one icon set. See [UI visual-direction guide](GUIDES/platforms/ui.md#light-expressive-starting-point) and [S7](#source-evidence).
- **Interaction and accessibility checks.** The empty state explains how to start and where text goes. Pending status belongs to the active turn and is announced without moving keyboard focus. Error persists beside the composer with the recovery action. Preserve the reading position when the user has scrolled away from the latest turn, and keep focus visible on keyboard activation, with focus at the composer following New chat. Use motion only to explain a status or transition and honor reduced motion. Verify rendered empty, pending, reply and error states with keyboard and assistive technology, both palettes and representative narrow (320 CSS px browser width for the chat's responsive composition) and wide (1280 CSS px) checks. The chat must neither cause nor require horizontal scrolling, including with long unbroken text. External Salesforce shell behavior is a separate host check. Apply WCAG AA contrast to the provided UI, including 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and 3:1 for applicable non-text controls and focus indicators. Verify contrast and keyboard usability in the chosen org theme. Native Salesforce mobile is outside the default desktop-browser delivery surface. [S7](#source-evidence)
- **Operating conditions, capacity and cost.** Single development org, online browser, synthetic input and an enabled xAI team. One active send per chat and at most one outbound call per admitted send give observable load and cost controls, including across repeated Send. Salesforce documents a maximum cumulative callout timeout of 120 seconds and a Developer Edition cap of 20 concurrent external callouts. The selected org type, callout and browser deadlines, serialized payload/heap headroom, output allocation, team rate and spend constraints remain open. Verify actual org and model limits, test the adopted deadlines under controlled latency and measure representative synthetic sends before committing to any latency/SLA or throughput target. No production availability, load or budget target was supplied. [S5](#source-evidence)
- **Reliability, privacy and operability.** Verify no business-record reads or writes, no content persistence or app-owned payload logs, no credential leakage, isolation after reset, and containment on failure to activate or revoke access. Verify deployment identity and rollback with scoped metadata/grant read-back, and the on-page error and diagnostic categories with synthetic failures. The approved retention/region and support assumptions remain open under R-4. No additional durability target applies to the transient chat. [S2, S3, S6](#source-evidence)
