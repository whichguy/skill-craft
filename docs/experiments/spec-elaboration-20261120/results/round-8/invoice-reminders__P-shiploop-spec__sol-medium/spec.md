# Invoicing

## Scope and source

The 2026-11-21 user request, **The incoming request**, reads “Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.” The direct outcome is that an invoice recipient receives a reminder for an eligible unpaid invoice without the billing operator taking action for each reminder. The supporting outcome is that the automation follows the current invoice state and stops reminding when an invoice is no longer eligible.

**Evidence and starting point.** The standalone-stage brief, **Where you are**, supplies no product repository, earlier specification, discovery, research, selected baseline, reference system or runtime. The behavior below is proposed for acceptance. Existing billing behavior, capabilities and access remain unknown. There are no prior clauses available to reconcile and no parity requirements. Invoice issuance, payment collection and reconciliation, due-date edits, manual reminders and changes to customer records are outside this change. Activation with real recipients is outside this stage.

**Operating model.** The billing owner authorizes automatic reminders for its billing scope. The invoice authority supplies current issued-invoice, balance and billing-recipient facts. The reminder process acts under scoped billing authority independently of operator sessions. The authorized invoice recipient receives the reminder through the approved channel. Authorized billing operators can distinguish reminder outcomes at the supported billing operations surface, whose entry point is open. All case names below identify acceptance observations and future checks.

## Requirements

### R-1 Automatic eligibility and timing

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 user request, **The incoming request**, “automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.” Adds scheduled eligibility and suppression. Invoice issuance, balances, payment state, recipient, and due dates remain owned by invoicing. There is no inspected pre-change reminder behavior. Implementation and runtime checks are pending.

**Draft product rules.** A reminder slot is one occurrence in the billing scope's approved recurring policy. Eligibility requires an issued collectible invoice, positive outstanding balance, a reached due instant, a usable authorized billing destination and active reminder authority. Partial payment remains eligible while its authoritative balance is positive. Draft, void, canceled, or fully settled invoices are ineligible. An invoice can be evaluated from the slot opening instant inclusive until its closing instant exclusive. Slots for an invoice do not overlap. Activation and recovery evaluate open slots only. Pre-activation slots are excluded, and closed slots are skipped after downtime. Existing unpaid invoices can qualify at their next post-activation slot.

- **Eligible slot (F-1/T-1, Slot timing).** Positive — an eligible invoice enters processing automatically when its slot opens, even with all operator sessions closed. Failure — early or expired slots produce no outbound request. Boundary — the due and slot-opening instants are inclusive, and the closing instant is exclusive. Surface and confirmation — compare the clock, authoritative invoice fixture, scoped processing outcome and outbound requests before, at and after the boundary.
- **Activation and catch-up (F-1 and F-3/T-2 and T-7, Activation and catch-up).** Positive — after scoped activation, the next eligible slot can run for an invoice issued before activation. Failure — inactive scopes and slots that opened before activation produce no send. Boundary — restart can recover currently open, post-activation slots only. Surface and confirmation — compare outbound counts and scoped outcomes across an activation and a restart spanning a closed slot.
- **Unpaid filter (F-1/T-2, Eligibility).** Positive — issued invoices with a positive balance, including partial payments, qualify when the other guards pass. Failure — a valid ineligible record produces no send, and an absent or malformed required fact produces a distinguishable blocked reason without a send. Boundary — a zero balance suppresses the reminder. Surface and confirmation — vary only invoice status or balance per fixture and verify both the recorded reason and outbound request count.
- **Final authorization (F-2/T-4 and T-5, Payment race and Authority recovery).** Positive — authorize a dispatch only after revalidating current balance, collectibility, destination, scope and automation authority for that invoice and slot. Failure — if a payment clearing the balance, void, deletion, changed destination or revoked authority takes effect before authorization, the previously prepared send cannot proceed. A changed destination requires fresh evaluation using the new destination while the slot is open. Missing or stale authority or source state blocks dispatch until fresh facts are available in the open slot. Boundary — an outbound handoff already authorized and in flight may complete after a newer change. Later work always revalidates. Surface and confirmation — interleave each source change before and after authorization at the actual source/dispatch boundary and inspect the outbound recipient, request count and operator outcome.

**State and data.** The target is the invoice's canonical identity within its billing scope, together with the active reminder policy and that slot's scheduled occurrence. The invoice authority retains the invoice, balance, due data and recipient. The billing owner controls scope activation and policy. Only authorized scoped processes and operators may read the minimum necessary facts. Reminder processing reads these records without changing them. Use effective source ordering to resolve concurrent payment, recipient and permission changes against dispatch authorization. After a source outage, resume with a fresh eligible read in an open slot. Before and after evaluation the invoice records are unchanged and any reminder work remains scoped.

**Open gaps.** The billing owner must confirm the due-date interpretation and timezone, cadence, quiet hours, expiry, collection limits, first/last occurrence and treatment of existing overdue invoices. Policy for disputed, written-off, reopened and similarly exceptional invoices is unknown. Use the invoice source's collectible status when available and withhold records whose eligibility remains ambiguous. The source owner must identify the authoritative fields, effect of reversal and terminal statuses, scoped identity and access, and how to establish final authorization ordering with dispatch. A stale read with no ordering guarantee cannot satisfy the race rule. Production eligibility, rollout and dispatch ordering depend on those answers.

### R-2 Recipient reminder

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 user request, **The incoming request**, “reminders for unpaid invoices.” Adds a recipient-visible reminder using the invoice's billing destination and facts. The invoice record and its established access policy are preserved. There is no observed delivery or message baseline. Implementation, provider and recipient checks are pending.

- **Correct recipient (F-2/T-4 and T-6, Recipient delivery).** Positive — the authorized billing recipient can receive the automated reminder for the intended invoice. Failure — missing, invalid or unauthorized destination prevents dispatch and leaves an operator-visible reason. Boundary — equal invoice references in different billing scopes cannot affect targeting or reveal the other scope's records. Surface and confirmation — use approved destinations for two isolated scopes with the same invoice reference, inspect actual received messages and verify the absence of cross-scope submissions or receipts.
- **Correct message (F-2/T-4 and T-6, Recipient content).** Positive — the reminder identifies the billing sender and invoice reference, gives the approved outstanding amount, currency and due date from the final authorized invoice snapshot, and uses approved wording to prompt payment. Failure — missing or inconsistent required fields block dispatch with an operator-visible reason. Boundary — if the invoice is partially paid before dispatch authorization, the message uses the remaining balance. Surface and confirmation — compare the rendered notice received at an approved destination against the exact source values, including a changed balance case.

**State and data.** Recipient identity comes from the invoice authority at final authorization. The scoped reminder process and the selected delivery boundary see only the facts needed for that notification. Send the notice to that destination only. Operator outcomes expose minimal scoped identifiers, timestamps and reason codes. General diagnostics omit addresses, message bodies, credentials and payment details. Persistent copies of message content or address within reminder processing are unnecessary. The recipient receives the invoice details described in the acceptance line. The billing owner defines address and invoice-data deletion at their source and confirms message and metadata lifecycle with the delivery owner. Copies already sent are subject to the recipient's and provider's retention.

**Open gaps.** Email to the designated billing recipient with a short plain-text reminder is a draft default. Confirm channel, sender identity, disclosure and approved wording, applicable contact permissions and the response to opt-outs. If authority cannot be established, withhold delivery. Payment links, contact creation, group recipients and new recipient accounts are outside scope. Delivery route, supported read-back and authorized test destination are prerequisites to real delivery.

### R-3 Duplicate prevention, recovery and truthful outcomes

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 user request, **The incoming request**, “automatic reminders.” Adds recoverable background processing and outcome visibility as supporting product behavior. Billing mutations and existing authority remain with their owners. Prior event or operations coverage is unknown. Implementation and recovery, isolation and delivery-status checks are pending.

- **One logical reminder per slot (F-1 and F-2/T-1, T-4 and T-7, Duplicate work).** Positive — each eligible slot can produce a reminder while later independent slots may remind if the invoice still qualifies. Failure — repeated triggers, overlapping workers and recovery cannot produce additional accepted submissions for the same slot. Boundary — distinct invoice or slot identities are processed independently. Surface and confirmation — count accepted submissions by scope, invoice, and occurrence through overlapping and restarted execution, with an eligible later slot as control.
- **Recover definite failures (F-3/T-3, T-6 and T-7, Restart and retry).** Positive — accepted work is recoverable after interruption and a proven temporary rejection can retry the same logical reminder within the open slot after fresh authorization. Failure — permanent rejection ends that slot with a failure reason. Expired, ineligible or revoked work cannot retry. Boundary — at slot close, unsent work stops retrying and remains explainable. Surface and confirmation — interrupt before submission, restart, exercise definite temporary and permanent rejection and advance the slot clock. Inspect durable outcomes and all outbound requests.
- **Resolve uncertain handoff (F-3/T-6 and T-7, Unknown outcome).** Positive — if the authoritative delivery boundary later confirms acceptance, reconcile the same occurrence to accepted. Failure — if the outcome is ambiguous, preserve an unknown outcome and withhold re-submission unless authoritative evidence establishes nonacceptance and the slot is still open and eligible. Surface status stays unknown when reconciliation is unavailable. Boundary — after slot close, late evidence may reconcile an earlier attempt without opening another attempt. Surface and confirmation — interrupt after the boundary accepts but before the result becomes durable, restart and check for zero extra submissions and correct read-back before and after reconciliation.
- **Outcome visibility (F-3/T-6 and T-8, Outcome access).** Positive — authorized operators can distinguish pending, blocked, suppressed, skipped, failed, unknown and channel-accepted outcomes as applicable. Failure — revoked and other-scope actors cannot retrieve outcome details or infer their existence through the lookup response. Boundary — channel acceptance is reported as such. A delivered outcome requires affirmative delivery evidence for that same occurrence, where available. Late or duplicate evidence cannot regress an outcome or reopen a send. Surface and confirmation — compare authorized and unauthorized read-back to authoritative correlated status, including a channel acceptance without recipient-delivery evidence.

**State and data.** The reminder process owns durable work and minimal outcome state in the selected runtime's billing scope. The logical identity is scope + canonical invoice identity + scheduled occurrence. Retries share it. An attempt's correlation to channel evidence is distinct from that logical identity and from the invoice revision read for authorization. Before first claim there is no dispatch authority for that occurrence. Accept work only once it is recoverable. One current processing authority can progress a particular occurrence. Stale owners and out-of-order results cannot overwrite a newer outcome. Record the possibility of a handoff before calling the channel so restart can distinguish safe continuation from ambiguous submission. Required operator read-back stays available even if diagnostic collection is unavailable. Track only the scope, invoice and occurrence references, outcome, timing, safe reason and evidence correlation needed for recovery and review.

**End and removal.** An occurrence ends in suppression, expiry, permanent failure or channel acceptance, with delivery evidence recorded when supported. An ambiguous occurrence stops sending and may still be reconciled. Offboarding or revocation prevents new sends and restricts read-back immediately. Ended work and diagnostic records are purged under approved retention and deletion policy, with enough surviving evidence or a closed-slot rule to prevent replay. Reconciliation after deletion cannot restore protected data or create work. Recipient content stays off the reminder operations surface.

**Open gaps.** The runtime owner must confirm background execution, protected durable progress and scoped read-back, concurrency and restart guarantees, workload and quotas, retry interval and attempt ceiling, reconciliation scope, record lifecycle and how deletion preserves replay safety. The delivery owner must demonstrate which responses mean definitive acceptance or nonacceptance and which outcomes can be retrieved after interruption. Scope-limited planning can proceed while those proofs are pending. Real dispatch and recovery cannot be marked ready without the relevant contracts.

## Behavior flows and transitions

| Flow | Ordered interaction and result |
| --- | --- |
| F-1 Scheduled evaluation (R-1, R-3) | A time trigger gives the scoped reminder process an eligible evaluation window. It reads invoice and policy facts and accepts recoverable work (T-1) or skips, suppresses or blocks (T-2). |
| F-2 Dispatch (R-1, R-2, R-3) | The processor takes responsibility for pending work (T-3), validates current authority and invoice contents (T-4/T-5) and hands off asynchronously if authorized. Channel evidence updates the correlated outcome (T-6). Recipient receipt is separately observable. |
| F-3 Recovery and read-back (R-3) | A later run recovers abandoned, blocked or retryable work, or reconciles ambiguous work against channel evidence (T-7). Authorized operations read-back uses current scope permissions (T-8). |

| Transition | Guard, effect and recovery | Acceptance locator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-1 Accept slot | Absent work, active scope, open slot and eligible invoice. Accept pending work durably for the logical identity. Duplicate evaluation joins the same outcome. An interruption before durable acceptance leaves evaluation repeatable. | Slot timing, Duplicate work |
| T-2 Exclude or defer | Before the slot opens there is no work. A closed or pre-activation slot is skipped. An inactive scope submits nothing. Known ineligibility suppresses that slot. Missing or stale facts block evaluation with a reason and may recover within the open slot. All branches submit zero notices. | Eligibility, Slot timing, Activation and catch-up |
| T-3 Claim work | A current processor claims pending or definitely retryable work. A competing or stale claim cannot authorize a handoff. Interrupted claims are recoverable through T-7. | Restart and retry |
| T-4 Authorize handoff | Current processing authority, current authorized source facts, eligible invoice, open slot and valid content. Record handoff intent and authorize that snapshot for submission. Uncertain interruption after intent goes to reconciliation. | Payment race, Recipient delivery, Recipient content |
| T-5 Withhold handoff | New ineligibility suppresses, expired slot skips, missing facts or unusable content block. A changed destination invalidates the prepared notice and may re-enter T-4 with current facts. No outbound request comes from the invalidated preparation. | Payment race, Authority recovery, Recipient content |
| T-6 Observe channel result | Correlated acceptance closes sending, definitive temporary rejection permits bounded reevaluation, permanent rejection fails, ambiguous response enters unknown. Affirmative delivery evidence may update accepted status. | Outcome access, Unknown outcome, Restart and retry |
| T-7 Recover or reconcile | Within the open slot, reclaim abandoned work before handoff intent, resume a cleared block, or retry definite nonacceptance through T-3 and T-4. Reconcile unknown with authoritative evidence. Without evidence keep unknown and send nothing. Closed, definitely unsent work skips. | Duplicate work, Unknown outcome, Restart and retry, Activation and catch-up, Authority recovery |
| T-8 Read outcome | An operator with current scoped access reads the latest outcome. Unauthorized lookup reveals no scope-external details. Read-back changes no work. | Outcome access |
