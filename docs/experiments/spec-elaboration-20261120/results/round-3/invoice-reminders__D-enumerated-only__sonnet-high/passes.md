# Passes

The working directory held no code and no existing invoicing product, so the specification is written for a new reminder feature on top of an invoice record (status, balance, due date, currency, customer with billing emails). Where the request or the situation is silent, the clause says so as a choice.

## Pass 1 — the ground
Added U-1 to U-16: first-time set-up on existing data, configuring (with invalid values), the customer paying, partial payment, opt-out, several invoices to one recipient, pause/resume, send now, test send, observing, handling failures, turning off and on, the unattended system run, invoices changing mid-sequence, the invoice link, and a user without permission. Added the first clauses each needs (candidates, schedule, message, controls, history). Found nothing on: SMS or other channels (deliberately left out, C-89).

## Pass 2 — what each sentence takes for granted
"Unpaid" takes for granted a status set and a balance (C-1, C-2, C-3, C-88); "invoice" a due date (C-4); "reminders" an addressee that exists and is deliverable (C-28 to C-33), a schedule (C-5, C-6), a time of day and a time zone (C-8, C-9, C-10), a message with defined content, amounts, a way to pay and a link a customer without an account can open (C-38 to C-52), a language (C-43) and an opt-out (C-34 to C-37). "Automatic" takes for granted that someone can turn it on, off, observe it and cannot be locked out of correcting it (C-54 to C-72). Second-level questions on my own clauses added: what "deliverable" means (C-31), what "verified sending domain" means (C-90), what "start of eligibility" covers (C-14, dimension listed), what the final outcomes are (C-16, C-68), which reasons are shown (C-24, C-70, C-94).

## Pass 3 — where the parts meet
Payment arriving while a reminder waits (C-23, C-93); two runs at once and a repeated run (C-80); a run cut off (C-81); manual and automatic the same day (C-21); schedule edited mid-sequence (C-17); due date edited (C-18); time zone or send time edited (C-19); Off then On (C-58, C-59); pause and resume (C-60, C-61); opt-out while a reminder is queued (C-34); reopened invoice (C-27); an address added after steps passed (C-14, last sentence); catch-up after downtime collapsing to one message (C-15); several invoices to one recipient and overlap with the daily rule (C-46 to C-49); deferred sends meeting the next step (C-76); late bounce on a combined message (C-32); invoice link after the invoice changed (C-52).

## Pass 4 — the runtime's own rules
Email provider: authenticated sender before reminders can be switched on (C-90, C-44, C-56), one-click unsubscribe headers (C-53, unconfirmed whether mandatory for this volume), rate and daily quota (C-76, figures unconfirmed, 10/s decided), temporary vs permanent refusal vs unknown outcome (C-73, C-74, C-75). Scheduler: may run twice or be cut off, so at-most-once per step and resumable work (C-80, C-81), cadence (C-79), volume (C-82). Calendar: daylight-saving gap and overlap (C-12, C-13), calendar-day offsets and leap days (C-10). Shipping beyond development: no real customer mail from non-production (C-83). Data protection: erasure (C-86) and retention (C-72), both with unconfirmed legal figures.

## Pass 5 — what must not change and must never happen
Invoices untouched by sending (C-85); opt-out does not alter the debt (C-36); one business never sees another's data (C-84); a link never opens another invoice (C-50); a step never sends twice (C-16, C-80); a settled invoice is never reminded beyond the unavoidable in-transit window (C-23, C-93); a rule enforced in the automatic path is enforced in the manual path (C-63); one failure does not stop the others (C-78); roll-back leaves data as it was (C-87).

## Pass 6 — grind
Read the whole specification as the builder. Fixed: a retry example with wrong times (C-73); a garbled currency example (C-41); an example for C-14 that contradicted its own rule; a dangling cross-reference (C-35 pointed at the wrong clause; C-16 at C-69); two clauses that had two behaviours (the disregard line was split out of C-38 into C-93; the Uncollectible choice was split out of C-2 into C-88; the manual-send-while-paused choice was split out of C-63 into C-95; the display precedence of reasons was split out of C-70 into C-94); a clause that mixed "implied" and "choice" sources (C-2, C-9, C-20, C-38, C-63, C-66, C-70); a Disputed invoice state that would have forced a new state onto the invoice model and duplicated Pause (removed; Pause serves disputes); a missing definition of "deliverable" (C-31) and "verified" (C-90); a missing complete list of start-of-eligibility events (C-14) and the missing per-address event; a missing bound on payment instructions (C-40); an Off/Off-then-On inconsistency between C-58 and C-59; combining across runs (C-46) and the 20-invoice cap (C-47); the time zone default (C-9).

## Pass 7 — the pass that found nothing a builder would have got wrong
Read every use case against its clauses and every clause against the questions of passes 2 to 5. Looked for: an Observable that cannot be observed; a dimension named without its members (outcomes, reasons, events that move eligibility, states that stop reminders, controls, message parts, header fields all list their members); a bound without a number (schedule 1 to 10 offsets, -60 to +365; recipients 5; combined 20; retries 3 at 15 min, 1 h, 4 h; test sends 20 an hour; link validity 90 days; retention 24 months; payment instructions 1,000 characters; throughput 10 a second; 10,000 due reminders in 6 hours; run interval 30 minutes; delay 60 minutes; erasure 30 days); a piece of state with no end (opt-out cleared by operator C-37; suppression cleared C-33; pause resumed C-61; history retained then bounded C-72); a use case with no unhappy path (each has one: U-2 invalid values, U-8 refusals C-63, U-11 failures, U-15 bad link, U-16 refusal); a silent decision (each is marked choice). Pass 7 changed only the cross-reference in C-76 to C-15 and the bounced-on-every-row wording in C-32, and found nothing else. A final read for wording-only tidying was not counted as a pass.

Deliberately left out and not specified: operator notification email/digest when a sequence ends unpaid, escalation or late fees, replies handling beyond Reply-To, per-customer or per-invoice schedules, weekend or holiday rules, multiple languages, SMS and other channels, attachments, a copy of each reminder to the operator.
