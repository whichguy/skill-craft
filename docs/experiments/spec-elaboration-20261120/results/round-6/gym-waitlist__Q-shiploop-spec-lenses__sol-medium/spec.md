# Class Booking App

## Basis and scope

User request, 2026-11-20, “The incoming request”: “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” The request establishes a class booking app and the possibility of a full class. It does not establish the app's roles, policies, data, screens, integrations, or runtime. This standalone stage has no prior spec, discovery, research, selected baseline, repository, or reference system for parity. The preservation clauses below are obligations to check against the actual app. The product defaults are assumptions for this specification. No existing requirement IDs were available to reconcile.

This feature applies to individual scheduled class occurrences and the existing booking identity. A member who currently has no seat can opt in when the occurrence is full, keep or withdraw their place, and receive an ordinary booking if a seat becomes available and they still qualify. There is no guarantee a seat will open. No changes to class discovery, guest or group booking, memberships, billing policy, or the existing cancellation policy are in scope.

## Requirements

### R-1 — Join the waitlist for a full class

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-20 request, “The incoming request,” “add a waitlist” and “full classes.” Preserve existing occurrence visibility, ordinary booking for available seats, booking restrictions, and existing reservations. Add one active entry per eligible booking identity per full occurrence, with explicit consent to ordinary automatic booking. Baseline routes and policy are unobserved. Implementation and product checks are pending.

| Acceptance | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-1.a — Discover the action | On a visible, open, full occurrence with waitlisting enabled, a member eligible except for capacity can choose **Join waitlist**. Before joining, explain that joining does not reserve a seat, a seat can be booked automatically under the ordinary booking terms, and the member can leave while waiting. | For a visible closed, cancelled, paused, or otherwise ineligible occurrence, present the applicable own-action restriction and no Join action. For missing or inaccessible occurrences, use the app's unavailable response without revealing restricted details. | With an unallocated seat and no waiting priority, the surface offers ordinary booking. A member already booked sees their booking. A waitlisted member sees their own status and Leave. | Existing class booking surface. Compare rendered state, available actions, and accessible action labels across these cases. |
| R-1.b — Commit a join | An authenticated eligible member acting for their own booking identity creates one waiting entry for the selected occurrence. It is appended in authoritative acceptance order. Confirm **Waitlisted** only after commit. | Reject malformed or missing occurrence, expired authentication, unauthorized target identity, an unavailable occurrence, or a failed eligibility check without creating an entry or booking. Service failure follows R-4.b. | First join establishes the head. A repeated or simultaneous join for the same pair returns the active entry without moving it. Different occurrences of the same class are independent. If a free seat with no prior waiting priority exists at commit, return current availability and offer ordinary booking. **State and data.** The booking authority owns the occurrence and booking identity and is the source for the active entry. Its logical entry data consists of occurrence ID, booking identity, a distinct entry identity, committed order, status, the recorded opt-in and applicable booking terms reference, and when applicable the end reason or confirmed booking reference. The opt-in applies only to the occurrence and terms agreed at join. Retrying a join attributes the result to the same entry or intent, even if that entry has since ended. R-2 defines when a fresh intent is allowed, and R-4 defines recovery. A booked member has no active entry for the same occurrence, and an active entry requires an accepted join. No member sees other entries or a queue roster. The first admissible join is allowed on an empty queue. **Open gaps.** Product and booking owners must establish the canonical actor and occurrence identifiers, class lookup and permission paths, eligibility predicates and refusal precedence, and supported terms that can be presented at consent. If the app can't establish informed consent for an ordinary booking, auto-booking for those classes is unready. The largest supported queue, any admission cap and the runtime limits behind it are unresolved. If an evidenced cap applies, the next join is refused with a clear reason and leaves existing entries and bookings intact. No policy tiers, waitlist position estimates, or extra identity fields are introduced.

**State and data.** The booking authority owns the occurrence, effective capacity and confirmed bookings. It controls which entries can become bookings. An active entry does not consume capacity. While actively prioritized, it prevents unrelated bookings from taking capacity pending its evaluation. Each committed promotion leaves exactly one ordinary booking for the booking identity and closes exactly that waiting entry. Closing or paused classes, where relevant, are checked before booking, including in retry and recovery. Each ordinary booking effect that applies to a promoted booking must occur under its existing authority, with duplicates and incomplete effects reconciled under R-4. **Open gaps.** Booking owner, before seat-allocation integration: identify every capacity and write path (member, staff and integrations, if present), meaning of effective capacity, authority for eligibility and definitive versus retryable refusal, cutoff and clock/time zone, what constitutes a material changes event in the app, and side effects such as credit, payment, or booking confirmation. Existing billing/refund and cancellation terms take precedence. A conflict between an automatic booking and an existing requirement for fresh affirmative action, or an authorized legacy path that bypasses capacity/priority, leaves the affected route unready pending resolution. Waitlist-only holds with expiry, membership-based priority, reserved staff seats invented by this feature, and overbooking are out of scope.

The applicable execution/time limits, retry policy, reconciliation horizon and recovery bounds are unresolved. ## Submission

**summary** — The specification covers the member path from a full class to joining, checking status, leaving or receiving an ordinary booking, plus booking integrity, privacy and recovery.
