# Class Booking App

## Scope and evidence

The 2026-11-20 incoming request, under **The incoming request**, says “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” It establishes an existing class booking product and two intended outcomes: a member can stay in contention when a class is full, and that waitlist can turn an opening into a booking. The second outcome is inferred from the stated purpose. No product behavior was observed. This standalone stage was supplied no repository, prior spec, discovery or research result, selected baseline, runtime, or reference system. Existing eligibility, booking, cancellation, identity, and class-lifecycle rules must be recovered before integrating the affected paths. Missing evidence establishes neither parity nor absence.

The bookable unit is one scheduled class occurrence. A member uses the existing signed-in booking context. The proposed flow covers joining, checking or leaving one's own waitlist, and getting booked when a spot opens. Staff controls, external notifications, visible queue positions, a new identity flow, and a redesign are outside this increment. Product defaults below are assumptions subject to the named gaps.

## Requirements

### R-1 — Join the waitlist for a full class

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-20 request, **The incoming request**, “Add a waitlist” and “full classes don't turn people away.” Preserve ordinary booking wherever a seat is bookable and preserve existing eligibility. Add a self-service waitlist action in the full-class booking flow. No existing UI, rules, permissions, or write contract could be inspected. Implementation and TC-1–TC-3 are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-1.a | An eligible member without a booking for the occurrence can join when all capacity is booked or held for earlier waiters. Joining confirms Waiting only after the entry is committed and consumes no booking capacity. | An invalid occurrence, ended booking window, unauthorized member, or failed write creates no new entry. The member sees the applicable unavailable or retryable outcome. | If a stale Full view submits Join after a seat becomes directly bookable, show the updated Book action and create neither an entry nor a booking. | Existing class booking surface and authoritative member read-back. Confirm the entry and unchanged bookings, failed joins, and stale Full branch (TC-1, TC-2). |
| R-1.b | Repeat or concurrent joins by the same member for the same occurrence resolve to the same active entry and queue order. | A lost response is an unknown outcome until a fresh authorized read or retry resolves it. | An already-booked member sees the booking and cannot create an entry. Joining one occurrence leaves another occurrence, even of the same class, independent. | Booking authority and class view. Overlap joins and lose one reply, then compare active entry count, order, and occurrence isolation (TC-3). |

**Domain rules and state.** Use the existing booking authority for the occurrence and member identity, plus an authoritative waitlist entry scoped by that pair. Before an accepted join, the member has neither an active entry nor a valid booking for that occurrence. Afterward exactly one active entry exists with a stable order assigned on commit. A rejection leaves the occurrence, bookings, and other entries intact. A member who leaves and later rejoins starts at the tail. Current authoritative state decides eligibility and Full at the write, including replay after a response is lost. See R-4 for visibility, deletion, and runtime placement.

**Open gaps.** The booking owner must recover the occurrence identifier, current member/tenant scope, full-class entry point, eligibility rules, and booking window from the actual app before wiring or verifying joins. The proposed admission cases can be prepared independently.

### R-2 — Turn an opening into a booking

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-20 request, **The incoming request**, “so full classes don't turn people away.” Preserve ordinary booking validity, capacity, and cancellation semantics. Add priority for existing waiters on a bookable opening. The proposed default is first-in, first-out automatic booking, with that consequence explained before Join. No scheduling, payment, consent, entitlement, cancellation, or dispatch behavior was available for inspection. Implementation and TC-4–TC-7 are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-2.a | When a committed cancellation or other supported change creates bookable capacity, the earliest eligible waiting member gets a regular booking for that occurrence. Booking creation and removal from the active waitlist take effect together. | A failed promotion creates no partial booking and leaves a waiting member's order intact for recovery. A committed cancellation survives promotion failure. | For multiple openings, allocate only up to available capacity, in committed join order. A competing direct booking can use only capacity left after earlier waiters are accounted for. | Ordinary booking and waitlist read-back for affected members, plus booking-authority counts and ordering (TC-4, TC-5). |
| R-2.b | Recheck eligibility and the occurrence's booking window at allocation. Remove a definitively ineligible entry from contention, show the member an appropriate unavailable state, and consider the next waiter. | If eligibility cannot be determined, keep the affected entry in order and recover before allocating past it. | If the occurrence closes or is cancelled before allocation, stop promotion and end its remaining active entries. A later retry cannot revive them. | Member's booking/class surfaces and authoritative state. Change eligibility and close the occurrence during allocation (TC-6). |
| R-2.c | A committed opening that still needs allocation remains recoverable by the booking authority without a member screen open. After recovery, members read the committed result in the app. | A timeout or interruption leaves allocation pending when its outcome is unknown. Until reconciled, ordinary booking cannot consume a seat owed to an earlier waiter. | Duplicate or stale work rechecks current state, including any newer leave, closure or promotion, and cannot issue a second booking or exceed capacity. | Actual booking boundary and normal member read-back after interruption, restart and repeated processing (TC-7). |

**Domain rules and state.** The booking authority owns occurrence capacity, bookings, and the order shared by booking and waitlist changes. Opening, join, leave, close, and allocation decisions must agree for each occurrence. The opening must remain discoverable for allocation across interruption, or allocation must already be committed. Only a committed booking consumes a seat. A single allocation either creates one booking and ends one active entry or preserves both prior states. Waitlist allocation itself may never take occupancy above the authorized capacity. Subsequent capacity changes follow the existing booking policy, including its treatment of already-issued bookings. A direct booking request for a seat held for allocation receives updated availability and the applicable waitlist action. A seat becomes directly bookable if no earlier waiter can take it. Current eligibility and authority govern the eventual write, including loss of access between join and allocation.

**Open gaps.** The product owner must confirm that joining can authorize automatic booking under existing payment, credit, consent, eligibility, and cancellation rules. If new consent or a transaction the app cannot complete on the member's behalf is required, settle the allocation policy before freezing this slice. The booking owner must locate every supported writer of bookable capacity, establish the closing rule, and prove that allocation and recovery can honor priority across those writers. If existing policy requires immediate direct sale of a released seat or conflicts with the proposed priority, the allocation and booking integration remain unready. Join/status modeling can proceed independently.

On a lost reply, a member can recover by reopening the occurrence or retrying safely. R-4 governs read access and data lifecycle.

**Open gaps.** The app owner must identify existing surfaces, UI primitives, design tokens and appearance policy before composition, including where a closed or unavailable occurrence can be read. ## Non-functional requirements

- **Interface quality and accessibility.** Integrate the feature into the existing booking UI using a consistent spacing and type scale, semantic palette with light and dark themes, and one icon set. Design the empty state (no active waitlist or booking), loading, error, waiting, and booked states. Keep status and the next valid action adjacent to the class identity and time. The smallest and largest supported widths must show the class and all actionable status without horizontal overflow or clipped controls. Use visible keyboard focus, meaningful control names, accessible status announcements, and WCAG AA contrast. Keep focus and reading position stable through updates. Use motion only to explain a state change and honor reduced motion. Verify through rendered narrow and wide layouts in both themes, keyboard and touch paths, contrast measurements, assistive-technology status checks and reduced-motion review. This is due at system-test with the UI owner. Supported widths, existing theme policy and primitives are unverified and due before UI integration.
- **Visual direction.** Existing app identity, tokens and platform conventions lead. For a new graphical portion with no established treatment, start with the light Google Gemini reference and Material 3 Expressive principles from the [Light expressive starting point](../GUIDES/platforms/ui.md#light-expressive-starting-point). Use a light-first web appearance with a dark option where the app has no precedence-setting policy. Use tonal rounded surfaces, clear type hierarchy and a restrained accent to distinguish the availability and next-action area. Verify the rendered treatment with the UI criteria above. Planning selects the concrete treatment after inspecting the app.
- **Integrity and resilience.** Under competing joins, bookings and leaves on the same occurrence, and under lost replies, interrupted allocation and restart, demonstrate R-1 through R-4 invariants on the selected booking boundary. During service loss, display an unconfirmed state with a recovery path. The booking owner verifies these properties at system-test. Bounds for allocation delay, recovery effort, hot-occurrence concurrency and ordinary load are unresolved. Derive them from the supported runtime, actual quotas, existing service expectations and workload before sizing or making timing promises.
- **Privacy and operability.** Under foreign, changed and revoked member contexts, verify data isolation, safe and restricted diagnostic retrieval, and policy-driven cleanup at the actual authority and approved destination. Runtime log and data retention, applicable compatibility constraints, and existing audit requirements are unresolved under R-4. The booking and operations owners verify these at system-test. Any environment-only obligation follows the due-phase policy below. External notification delivery and its reliability targets are inapplicable because status is read in the app.

## Behavior model and specification replay

### Flows and transitions

| Flow and source | Transition sequence and decision points | Consumer result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-1 Join (R-1, R-3, R-4) | Member opens one occurrence, then submits Join. T-1 checks current authority, scope, eligibility, booking window and bookable capacity. T-2 commits one new entry or returns existing Waiting for a duplicate. An already-booked, stale Full, denied, invalid or failed request follows T-1's guarded alternative. | Waiting after commit, Book when a direct opening exists, or applicable booked/unavailable/retry state. |
| F-2 Allocate (R-2, R-4) | T-3 commits an authorized capacity-producing change and preserves the need for allocation if work remains. T-4 rechecks occurrence state and earliest waiter, ends a definitively ineligible entry or commits booking and ends an eligible entry together. T-5 reconciles an interrupted or duplicate attempt using current state. T-6 ends active entries on withdrawal or lifecycle closure as applicable. A competing ordinary booking goes through T-9, which commits only if capacity remains after waitlist priority and existing eligibility checks. | Ordinary booking for the eligible member awarded the seat. Pending work remains recoverable and a closed occurrence produces no promotion. |
| F-3 Read and withdraw (R-3, R-4) | T-7 reads current own status under current authorization. T-6 commits withdrawal of an active entry, resolves repeated leave, or returns an intervening booking. T-8 applies only an in-scope current response to the member view. | Confirmed own Waiting, Booked or no active entry, with actionable feedback for read or mutation failure. |

No active entry is the initial state. Waiting is the only active state. Promotion, withdrawal, ineligibility and closure end the entry. Booking is a separate existing record and survives later waitlist operations. A new Join creates a distinct intent and may create a new entry. A retry of an older Join is the same intent, scoped to the original account and occurrence, and cannot recreate a later-ended entry. Retry of a Leave applies to the original withdrawal intent and cannot affect a newer entry. Intent retention, collision protection and any safe expiry behavior must be proven on the selected runtime under R-4. The allocation obligation exists only while a bookable opening still needs to be reconciled. Transient client cues and a retained ended entry cannot supply a seat or participate in allocation.

## Submission

**summary** — Specifies self-service waitlisting for full class occurrences, in-app status and withdrawal, and conversion of an opening into a booking. Assumptions are existing signed-in membership, occurrence-level identity, automatic booking in committed join order for eligible members, in-app read-back, and no visible position or external notification. An open material conflict concerns automatic booking and queue priority, which may conflict with existing consent, payment, eligibility or immediate direct-booking obligations. Allocation and dependent booking integration remain unready until resolved. Actual authority, runtime limits, retention, UI baseline and verification surfaces remain open for the affected work. Acceptance design and independent waitlist journey modeling can proceed. The replay used synthetic specification inputs. Product implementation and verification are pending.

**primary** — R-1 (stay in contention for a full class), R-2 (receive an eligible opening through the waitlist).
