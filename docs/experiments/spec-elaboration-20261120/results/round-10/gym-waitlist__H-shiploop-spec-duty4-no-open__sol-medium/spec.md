# Class Booking App Specification

## Source and scope

The product basis is the user’s 2026-11-21 message, under “The incoming request”:

> Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.

The request establishes an existing class booking app and the desired alternative to turning someone away when a class is full. An eligible customer can join the waitlist for that class and obtain a booking if a place becomes available in their turn. Joining is a chance to book if capacity opens.

No prior spec, discovery or research result, selected baseline, repository, runtime, or reference product was supplied. The current UI, booking routes, identity, policy, and deployment are unobserved. The preservation clauses below describe the intended compatibility with the app’s actual contract once recovered. The dated request authorizes the feature. Product defaults in this spec are inferred from that request and recorded under [Submission](#submission).

A **class** here is one scheduled occurrence. A **customer** is an existing booking principal authorized to act on the relevant class and booking. The **booking authority** owns capacity, bookings, and eligibility. The **waitlist authority** owns the queue and uses the booking authority’s rules to allocate places. These terms describe responsibilities. The runtime may fulfill both within the same boundary.

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, “Add a waitlist” and “full classes don't turn people away” in [Source and scope](#source-and-scope). Preserve existing class visibility, eligibility and normal booking while an unallocated place is available. Add the ability to opt in when capacity is the only booking blocker. Baseline behavior and prerequisites are unverified. Implementation and all acceptance checks are pending.

| R-1.a | An eligible customer with no booking or entry for an open, full class can select **Join waitlist** after seeing that an available seat may be booked automatically and that joining gives no guarantee of a seat. On commit, the customer has one active entry and no confirmed seat from joining. | A class the customer may not access or book, a canceled class, or a class closed to booking cannot be joined. **State and data.** Each active entry belongs to the booking scope, occurrence, and customer and carries the minimum information to prove opt-in and determine authoritative acceptance order. After acceptance there is one, with existing bookings and other classes unaffected. On a failed write there is no partial entry. Acknowledgment confirms durable state. On a lost response, use R-2 read-back. See R-3 and R-5 for removal.

**Bounds and prerequisites.** Existing rules for entitlements, class visibility, booking on behalf of another person, and eligibility are inherited wherever applicable. No new roles, group enrollment, or per-customer or per-class queue cap is introduced. Discovery of actual class identity, customer scope, authorization and the existing booking action is a prerequisite to wiring and checking joins. The application owner supplies those contracts before dependent implementation.

### R-4 Fill open seats from the waitlist

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, “so full classes don't turn people away” in [Source and scope]. No production release path, side-effect contract or concurrency capability was observed. Implementation and all acceptance checks are pending.

| Line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-4.a | When a confirmed cancellation releases a seat or a supported capacity change opens one, the booking authority automatically awards each available seat to the earliest active, currently eligible waitlisted customer. Consume that entry with the confirmed booking as one outcome. Show the ordinary confirmed booking on the next successful customer read. | Definitively ineligible entries are removed when reached and allocation proceeds in queue order. If eligibility or booking outcome cannot be determined, retain unresolved active entries and reconcile before assigning their seat to another customer. No status claims a booking until confirmed. | First-come, first-served by authoritative join acceptance order is the default. Concurrent joins get a definite order within the occurrence. Fill at most the number of available seats. At or above capacity nothing is allocated. With no eligible entry, available seats return to normal booking. | Real seat-release path, booking authority and affected customers’ booking views. Use two differently ordered waiters, an ineligible head, and one and multiple openings. Check exact booking owners, remaining queue order and occupancy. |
| R-4.b | Direct booking, waitlist allocation, withdrawal and booking closure respect one authoritative order for the affected occurrence. While an eligible waiter has priority or allocation is unresolved, a new direct booker cannot take that place. | Interrupted or duplicate release handling converges on the same valid allocation. A committed cancellation remains committed. Pending allocation survives interruption and is recovered by the booking authority independently of the client. A failed allocation creates neither a duplicate booking nor a lost active join. | There is at most one confirmed booking or one active entry per customer and occurrence. New allocation waits until confirmed occupancy falls below configured capacity. | Actual booking and release paths. Compete for the last freed seat, redeliver release handling and interrupt processing before commit and after commit but before acknowledgment. Read back bookings, availability, queue status and the cancellation result after recovery. |

**State and data.** The authority reconciles confirmed booking identities, released capacity, active entries and any unresolved allocation for that occurrence. Each seat is accounted for as occupied, available to ordinary booking or held for unresolved allocation before ordinary booking may use it. Durable release retains enough state to recover its allocation, if allocation is still pending. Authoritative completion changes the winning customer’s queue membership and booking consistently. Duplicate or delayed handling uses current state, not stale seat counts. The authority rechecks eligibility and the booking window when committing. Failure to establish eligibility preserves priority and never grants a booking. When the authority recovers, allocation is resolved only while the booking window remains open. R-5 governs closure.

**Bounds and prerequisites.** The automatic-booking default uses the explicit join consent and stays within an already authorized booking contract. It neither bypasses payments, waivers, conflicts or other preconditions nor assumes that join consent authorizes a new charge or obligation. Application and booking owners must identify all actual seat-release and direct-booking writers, current eligibility and booking side effects, and show that coordinated allocation and independent recovery are possible at those boundaries. If the existing rules require a new payment or separate confirmation when a seat opens, the product owner must decide the affected consent or acceptance flow before automatic booking is enabled there. That decision is conditional on discovery. Ordinary eligible, consent-compatible flows and status design do not depend on it.

## Submission

**summary**

The spec defines joining full classes, seeing and withdrawing one's own membership, automatically filling seats in order, and ending the queue when participation can no longer result in a booking. Existing booking contracts are preservation obligations. There is no sourced prior requirement to retire and no reference-system parity claim.

Assumptions by requirement:

| Requirement | Default and basis | Alternatives and revalidation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R-1 | One active entry per existing customer and class occurrence. An explicit join consents to automatic booking within established terms. Capacity alone makes an otherwise bookable class eligible for joining. No independent queue-size cap is set. These defaults keep one meaningful place in line per customer and avoid a second arbitrary cutoff for the full-class customer. | Group or priority reservations and a finite queue cap would require additional product rules. Revalidate against actual booking principal and occurrence semantics, consent obligations and confirmed runtime quotas before introducing any limit. |
| R-2 | Show own participation in the class view, use the existing confirmed-booking view, and refresh on entry, return, request or uncertain result. Omit queue position, separate history and external messages. This is enough to see the outcome and act on it without disclosing others or depending on another delivery path. | A live feed, position or outbound message could serve a documented freshness or communication need. Revalidate when the product's existing contracts or owner establish such a need. |
| R-3 | Withdrawal ends the specific active entry at commit. The existing cancellation path applies after booking. This preserves explicit withdrawal and protects newer joins. | An offer or separate withdrawal deadline would add another lifecycle. Revalidate if the allocation policy changes or the existing cancellation contract conflicts. |
