# Invoice Management

## Scope and source

**Request recorded 2026-11-21, “The incoming request”** — “Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.”

An authorized billing administrator enables reminders for a billing account. From then on, the product evaluates invoices in the background and sends eligible reminders to the invoice's authorized billing recipient. The invoice authority owns invoice facts, the reminder function owns scheduling and outcomes, and the selected delivery authority owns handoff receipts and any supported delivery updates. The billing administrator or authorized operator can confirm policy and outcome through the target's authorized configuration and operational surfaces. The recipient sees the reminder in the selected channel.

The inspected workspace supplied the request and ShipLoop guides. There is no prior accepted spec, repository, discovery or research result, selected baseline, or parity reference. Existing invoice semantics, configuration, integrations, and tools are unknown. No as-is behavior or platform capability was observed. Product decisions below are labeled as defaults where the request leaves the choice open. The only requested change is reminder automation. Invoice issuance, editing, payment processing, collections escalation, new identity or sharing, and a new in-product interface are outside this slice.
