# Class Booking App

## Scope and sources

**Request, 2026-11-21, “The incoming request”** — “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.”

The requested outcomes are that an eligible member can join a full class's waitlist (R-1, Join) and that waiting can turn into a confirmed place when a seat opens (R-2, Allocate). Leaving, class closure, and reading one's own status support those outcomes (R-3 and R-4). The scope is a scheduled class occurrence. A recurring series, priority tiers, staff reordering, guest/group queues, and outbound messages are excluded from this change. Members see their own outcome in the booking app.

**Observed evidence.** The standalone brief supplies the request and stage guides. There is no prior spec, selected baseline, discovery or research result, product repository, runtime, or reference system to compare. The requested outcome implies an existing class booking flow and a concept of full capacity. The existing flow, policy details, and rendered surfaces remain unobserved. The preservation clauses below describe compatibility obligations. The policies labeled defaults are proposed product decisions for unattended specification.

Confirmed occupancy cannot exceed bookable capacity as a result of this feature. A member cannot simultaneously hold an active wait entry and a confirmed booking for that occurrence. The proposed Join action states that the member will be booked automatically if a seat opens while they are still eligible. The default adds no claim window, new consent mechanism, new charge, notification channel, or priority override. ## Behavior model and service contracts

The member acts through the class booking app. The current booking authority, whatever the deployed boundary proves to be, decides identity, eligibility, capacity and state. The existing authorized booking member or class operator triggers seat-opening or closure through the applicable existing action. A class clock and lifecycle can close waitlisting while nobody has the app open. These are logical roles. The current runtime and the identity and permissions of its actual writers remain open in R-1 through R-4.

| Flow and transition | Ordered contract |
| --- | --- |
| F-1 / T-1, T-2 (R-1) | Member reads a full class, selects Join, and the authority rechecks access, occurrence state, eligibility, occupancy and member status. Valid input creates one active entry and confirms it. A repeated Join reconciles with existing status. A rejected or stale action reports the relevant current result and makes no new entry. |
| F-2 / T-3 (R-2) | Authorized action opens a seat. For an open occurrence with available eligibility decisions, the authority apportions bookable seats in queue order, ends allocated and definitively ineligible entries, and confirms the resulting bookings and remainder. Competing writes follow accepted order. The member reads committed status on return. |
| F-3 / T-4 (R-3) | Member submits Leave for their active entry. The authority checks ownership and applies the change to that entry only. On a competing promotion or a replay it returns the relevant current state without cancelling a booking or removing a newer entry. |
| F-4 / T-5 (R-3) | An authorized cancellation or the cutoff ends participation for that occurrence. At or after the cutoff the authority rejects joins and allocations even if active-entry cleanup has yet to finish. The app reads closure and hides the waitlist controls. |
| F-5 / T-6 (R-4) | Member opens, refreshes or returns to the class. The authority scopes its response to the authorized member and occurrence. The current view consumes the current authorized result. The view reconciles ambiguous changes by reading back status and discards responses for obsolete views or sessions. |

The authoritative member/occurrence states are **none**, **waiting**, **booked** (existing booking), and **ended without booking** (leave, closure or eligibility denial, with the cause visible only where authorized and useful). The first Join takes none to waiting, allocation takes waiting to booked, and Leave, denial or closure takes waiting to ended. A new eligible Join makes a new entry from ended. Booked members follow the existing booking lifecycle. The client pending state implies no membership change. Closure applies to the occurrence independently of the member state.

**App ↔ booking authority (R-1–R-4).** Reuse/extension candidate, with actual route unresolved. Verify current authority on each action and private read, including revocation without a booking edit. Successful acknowledgement represents committed state. Reads after an action, refresh or return reconcile current state and distinguish failure, unknown outcome and confirmed status. Changes target the affected occurrence, preserving unrelated data, bookings and consumers. Verify prior ordinary booking records still resolve, and no historical bookings are enrolled by enabling the feature. Validate lost responses, repeats, conflicting writes and session replacement at the actual selected app boundary. N/A for a cache/subscription contract because the specified interaction requires neither a cache nor a live subscription. If an existing one participates, establish freshness, revocation, stale-response and recovery rules before reuse.

**Seat and class lifecycle ↔ allocation (R-2–R-3).** Ownership and deployment boundary unresolved. Existing authorized actions or lifecycle signals supply seat and class changes. The booking authority owns the new booking effects. Only a current, authorized occurrence change can trigger them. Incremental effects are restricted to the targeted occurrence and remaining eligible seats. Repeated or reordered change inputs cannot restore closed participation or double allocate. Consumer read-back must agree with authoritative state even when no member screen is open. No remote worker, notification service, or independent lifecycle processor is presumed. Where discovery finds independently completing steps, confirm allowed authority after revocation, ownership and read-back of pending work, retry after interruption, and the rule preventing a stale completion before integrating the boundary.

**Owned event coverage (R-1–R-4).** Retain operational evidence of join, leave, promotion, closure, denied mutations, consequential write failures and recovery outcomes through approved existing coverage where it supports those outcomes. Use the authoritative owner as emitter, report occurrence and operation correlation only at the approved sensitivity, and verify operators can distinguish a confirmed change, a rejection and an unresolved failure. A failed evidence write cannot be presented as a change to booking state. Confirm emitter, destination, retention, access and applicable audit failure policy before selecting any change, then verify redaction, retrieval, and failure handling there. Identity-provider login success/failure remains with its current authoritative owner where applicable. No new authentication workflow or provider event is required.

## Submission

- **summary** — The specification covers joining a full class, allocating opened seats, leaving and closing the queue, and seeing one's own current status. Proposed defaults are occurrence-level self-service, first-in first-out automatic confirmation for eligible members, coherent seat allocation, in-app status, and a cutoff at the existing close time or the scheduled start if no close time applies. A rejoin goes to the tail, and reopening does not restore ended entries.
