# Class Booking App

## Scope and source

**Source S1** is the 2026-11-20 user message under “The incoming request”: “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.”

“Add a waitlist” and “full classes” map to R-1 A–C. “Don't turn people away” maps to R-3 A–C, which gives members a chance to get a freed seat. R-2, R-4 and R-5 cover withdrawal, lifecycle and trustworthy status. The scope is one scheduled class occurrence at a time, using the app's existing booking identity.

The request identifies an existing app. This standalone run provides the request and guides. No prior specification, selected baseline, repository, research, discovery or reference product was supplied. Preserved behaviors below are compatibility obligations to confirm at the actual product. There are no observations of its current implementation, policies, entry points or coverage. Product defaults are stated as assumptions in Submission.

## Requirements

### R-1 Join the waitlist for a full class

**Basis and delta.** S1, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-20, “Add a waitlist” and “full classes don't turn people away.” Preserve ordinary booking when a seat is available under existing policy. Add a way for eligible members to wait when there is no bookable seat because confirmed bookings occupy the bookable capacity. Actual admission rules and interfaces are unverified. Implementation and checks A–C are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Offer the next action | An eligible member with neither a booking nor an entry for an open, full occurrence sees **Join waitlist**. Before submission the app explains that joining may automatically book a later seat and makes no seat reservation now. | A closed or cancelled occurrence rejects join even from a stale screen and explains that joining is unavailable. | If a seat is ordinarily bookable after R-3 reconciliation, show the existing booking action. A stale join in that state returns that option and creates neither a booking nor an entry. If a seat has opened and priority is still being reconciled, a new join creates no entry and the screen indicates that availability is being updated, with a refresh action. | Member class screen and authoritative mutation result. Compare full, available and closed states for the same occurrence and submit from a stale view. |
| B. Confirm membership | A successful join creates one active entry for the member and occurrence. The app shows **Waitlisted** and a leave action, also visible on a fresh authorized visit. | A definitively failed join creates no entry or booking and gives reason-appropriate recovery guidance. This includes denial when current eligibility fails. | Joining leaves confirmed occupancy unchanged. Entries for other members or occurrences are unaffected. | Member class screen and scoped read-back. Reopen the class and compare the member's entry and occupancy before and after. |
| C. Make join safe to repeat | After a lost response, retrying the same intent or reading current status resolves the outcome without duplicating the entry. | An already-booked member receives their current booked state and gains no entry. | Double submission and simultaneous joins by the same member yield at most one entry and preserve its original order. Replaying an old join after leave cannot recreate it. | Member view and booking authority. Interrupt after commit, repeat the intent, then leave and replay it. Verify membership count and order through later allocation. |

**State and recovery.** The booking authority owns eligibility, occurrence status, capacity, bookings and entry state. Join targets the existing member and occurrence. An open, full occurrence and a currently eligible member with no active entry or booking can transition from absent to waiting without changing occupancy. The authority resolves competing capacity, closure and join changes against current state. R-5 governs uncertain outcomes and authorized read-back.

**Domain rules and open gaps.** The booking owner must inspect how bookable capacity, cutoffs, admission eligibility and the booking screen work before integrating or verifying admission at the target. If the app cannot honor the disclosed allocation for a class type, the product owner must settle its join availability and wording before exposing the action for that type. Membership and outcome modeling can proceed independently.

### R-2 Leave the waitlist

**Basis and delta.** S1, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-20, “Add a waitlist.” Add withdrawal as a product default that lets members withdraw consent while waiting. Preserve the rules for cancelling a confirmed booking. No actual cancellation surface or authority was inspected. Implementation and checks A–B are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Withdraw a pending entry | A member can leave their active entry. Confirmation and fresh read-back show they are no longer waiting, and that entry cannot later receive a seat. | A definitively failed withdrawal leaves the active entry intact and gives recovery guidance. An uncertain result follows R-5 B. | Repeating a leave has no further effect. A late leave for an old entry cannot remove its replacement. Rejoining while full and eligible starts at the back. | Member screen and authoritative status. Leave, repeat, rejoin behind another member and replay the old leave. Observe who gets the next seat. |
| B. Resolve a leave against allocation | If leave commits first, the seat goes to the next qualifying entry or becomes ordinarily bookable under R-3. | If allocation commits first, leave returns the current booked state with access to existing booking controls and does not cancel the booking. | In both commit orders the member is never both booked and waiting for that occurrence. | Control both commit orders at the booking authority and verify the member-visible result, exact seat holder, entry and occupancy. |

**State and recovery.** The member acts on the entry they own and saw. A successful leave makes that entry inactive and changes no booking. It resolves against allocation and closure at the authority. After interruption retry the original intent or read current status.

**Domain rules and open gap.** The booking owner must identify existing booking controls and restrictions, including whether a newly confirmed booking can be cancelled, before wiring the booked-state recovery action.

### R-3 Allocate freed seats to waiting members

**Basis and delta.** S1, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-20, “so full classes don't turn people away.” The proposed default is automatic booking, disclosed at join, with first-joined priority among eligible entries. Preserve existing capacity, eligibility, booking and cancellation obligations. Add priority when seats become bookable following accepted booking and capacity changes. Existing rules, seat writers and recovery capabilities are unverified. Implementation and checks A–D are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Allocate in order | While booking is open, each newly bookable seat goes to the oldest eligible active entry for that occurrence. The booking and completion of the entry take effect together. The promoted member sees **Booked** and ordinary booking details on return, even if the app was closed when the seat freed. | A withdrawn entry cannot receive a seat. A definitively ineligible entry ends without a booking and the next eligible entry can receive the seat. | If multiple seats open, allocate in order up to available capacity or until no eligible entry remains. Remaining entries keep their order. | Member booking views and authoritative records. Free seats while member views are closed and return as promoted and remaining members. Compare exact bookings and remaining order. |
| B. Protect capacity and priority | A seat with no eligible waiters after reconciliation returns to ordinary booking under existing rules. | A direct booking cannot bypass an eligible waiter. While the outcome is pending the new requester sees availability being updated, with a refresh action. Indeterminate eligibility neither authorizes a booking for that entry nor permits bypassing it. | Competing releases, direct bookings, repeated release signals and allocations cannot overbook or book any one member twice. A full occurrence has no allocation transition. | Exercise real seat changes and direct booking at the booking boundary. Verify exact seat holders and remaining entries under contention. |
| C. Recover interrupted allocation | The booking authority reconciles a committed release after interruption without requiring member interaction. Authoritative state resolves a pending acknowledgement. | Before allocation commit, failure exposes neither a booking nor entry completion. After commit, loss of acknowledgement preserves the booking and reprocessing recognizes it. | Repeated or stale work cannot undo newer outcomes. After restart, use current occurrence state and eligibility. | Interrupt and restore the booking authority before and after commitment. Check durable outcomes, operator-visible recovery and fresh member status. |
| D. Preserve booking obligations | An allocated booking follows the ordinary booking rules for that occurrence and member. | Allocation cannot bypass a booking guard or impose a new automatic charge or commitment beyond established consent. | If advance consent is insufficient for a class type, automatic allocation for that type remains unready pending an approved consent and allocation rule. | Actual booking boundary and member record. Inspect existing consent and payment effects and exercise a representative eligible and restricted case. |

**State and recovery.** The booking authority resolves concurrent joins in a deterministic commit order and owns occupancy, eligibility, allocation and recovery. Allocation requires an open occurrence, a bookable seat and a currently eligible head entry after definitive exclusions. It resolves that entry and creates its booking as one consistent outcome. A capacity reduction follows existing policy for incumbent bookings. While eligibility or release reconciliation is pending, any affected seat stays subject to queue priority. The authority keeps committed releases recoverable and retries against current state. Exhausted recovery leaves an operator-visible outstanding allocation and a supported path to reconcile it.

**Domain rules and open gaps.** Product and booking owners must confirm admission and allocation eligibility, definitive disqualification, membership and time conflicts, payment and other consent, and any existing priority or reserved-seat rules. The booking owner must find every seat writer, confirm when seats become bookable, and verify interruption recovery and the applicable completion window. These gaps gate affected allocation and release integration. Join and leave interaction design can proceed. Claim windows, manual offers, configurable priority, and outbound notifications are outside scope. Member status is available in-app.

### R-4 End waiting on closure or definitive disqualification

**Basis and delta.** S1, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-20, “full classes” and “waitlist.” Preserve the handling of confirmed bookings on closure and cancellation. Add termination of active entries when the occurrence is cancelled, reaches final booking closure or ends, or a definitive eligibility check fails at allocation. No scheduling or retention practices were inspected. Implementation and checks A–B are pending.

| Acceptance line | Positive | Failure | Boundary | Consumer surface and confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Close a waitlist | When the occurrence is cancelled, finally closes for booking or ends, waiting ends. Returning members can see the occurrence status and that their entries are inactive. | Delayed release work cannot allocate on a closed occurrence or reopen an entry. | If allocation commits first, the booking follows ordinary closure or cancellation policy. If closure commits first, no allocation occurs. | Member class and booking views and authoritative read-back. Compare both orders with an ordinary booking as a compatibility control. |
| B. End a disqualified entry | A member can see that definitive ineligibility ended waiting and receives a safe explanation of their own outcome. | A timeout or indeterminate eligibility cannot end the entry on that basis. | n/a: the definitive-versus-indeterminate boundary is covered by the positive and failure expectations. | Member status and booking authority. Compare those two conditions for the head entry and check the resulting booking and entry states. |

**State and recovery.** The schedule and booking authority own occurrence status and membership. Closure ends priority immediately at the authoritative boundary even if physical cleanup is deferred. Withdrawn, allocated, disqualified and closed entries are inactive. Delayed work reconciles to current terminal state. R-5 governs retention and deletion.

**Domain rules and open gaps.** The schedule owner must establish cutoff, cancellation and schedule-change semantics, including whether the same identity can reopen after final closure. An authorized fixture and existing booked-member outcomes are prerequisites for lifecycle integration and verification. The spec assumes no automatic transfer or reinstatement.

**Boundary contract and open gaps.** The member surface sends authorized reads or intents to the existing booking authority and receives scoped status. Current authorization governs each read and mutation. Stale display provides no write authority. Read-back after confirmation reports current state. Committed seat release and completed allocation are separately observable under R-3. Line C covers owned business events. A separate external delivery boundary is inapplicable because status is in-app. The booking and privacy owners must confirm actual service routes, roles and revocation behavior, supported storage and recovery capabilities, runtime quotas and approved retention and purge before dependent work. The operations owner must verify event retrieval and extend existing coverage only for demonstrated gaps. No additional identity, roster sharing or contact persistence is needed.

**Interaction and component premises.** Extend the class booking surface with the class status, next valid action and private membership status. Use established accessible controls once identified. Preserve class and action context while pending. Keep actionable errors visible and announce state changes. If a control disappears, retain focus on the status or next valid action. The interface criteria below govern the visual treatment.

## Non-functional requirements

- **Interface and accessibility.** Use a deliberate hierarchy with the class and next valid action as focal points, consistent spacing and type scales, one icon set and a coordinated light and dark palette. Design loading, empty, ended, error and confirmation states in that system. Verify the rendered flow at supported narrow and wide widths with no horizontal overflow, including keyboard and touch use, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast and accessible status announcements in both themes. Use motion only to clarify a change, with equivalent feedback when reduced motion is requested. Extend the product's visual identity and platform conventions once identified. Where new visual choices are open, the named reference is light Google Gemini using Material 3 Expressive principles. Start with airy tonal surfaces, friendly precise type and a prominent primary action, with a dark option. The product's appearance policy and supported widths are open before concrete design verification. [UI visual direction](../GUIDES/platforms/ui.md#light-expressive-starting-point)
- **Integrity and recovery.** During contention on one occurrence, duplicate actions, authority restarts and temporarily unavailable eligibility or status, satisfy the capacity, priority, authority and consistency invariants in R-1 through R-5. Verify by controlling interleavings and failure points and inspecting member and authoritative read-back. The operations owner must confirm recovery obligations and any numeric completion deadline under R-3.
- **Performance, freshness and quotas.** Evaluate join, leave, status and release processing under contention on a popular occurrence and on independent occurrences. Verify truthful pending presentation and successful reconciliation after resource pressure. Latency, throughput, queue size, availability, cost and runtime quotas are unresolved. The booking and operations owners must establish supported workloads and deployed execution, storage and request limits, and identify any existing performance targets before quota-dependent work. No external delivery performance criterion applies because the outcome is available in-app.
- **Privacy and compatibility.** Verify R-5 on real client and operational boundaries, including protected response bodies and account changes. Verify direct booking, cancellation and class closure with pre-existing booking data and unaffected occurrences. The actual target, retention constraints and any migration or cutover needs remain open.
