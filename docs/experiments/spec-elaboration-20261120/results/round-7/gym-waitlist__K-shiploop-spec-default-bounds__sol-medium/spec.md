# Class Booking App

## Scope and basis

**Product source** — “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” User request, 2026-11-21, “The incoming request.”

The request establishes an existing class booking app and the need for a path when a class is full. The product outcomes are a way to join that class's waitlist (R-1.A–C) and a way for a queued member to get a seat that opens up (R-3.A–C). R-2 lets a member withdraw consent. R-4 and R-5 make the result private, durable and visible in the app.

The product source establishes intent. The standalone-stage instructions establish that no earlier spec, discovery, research or selected baseline is available. No product screen, booking rule, runtime, consumer interface or deployed behavior was observed. References to existing behavior below are preservation requirements or integration prerequisites. The request implies a seat-capacity concept. It supplies no external product reference or parity target.

A **class** means one scheduled occurrence, even if it is part of a recurring series. A **member** is the existing identity entitled to book for themself. The booking authority determines class identity, effective capacity, confirmed seat occupancy, eligibility and time at the point of decision. An **eligible** member meets the ordinary booking rules other than the absence of a seat when joining. A class is **full** when confirmed occupancy equals effective capacity. **Cutoff** is the earlier of the class start and any existing booking close applicable to that class. When the app has no booking close, cutoff is the class start. The waitlist window also respects the existing booking opening rule and other existing closures. At the cutoff instant the window is closed.

## Requirements

### R-1 Join a full class's waitlist

**Basis and delta.** Product source, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-21. Preserve class discovery, ordinary booking for an available seat and existing identity, eligibility and capacity rules. Add an explicit opt-in for a full occurrence and confirmation of the member's own waiting state. The controls, limits and policy below are defaults under Submission. The baseline is unavailable. Implementation and consumer/service checks are pending.

- **R-1.A Admission.** Positive — an eligible member with neither a booking nor an active entry can choose **Join waitlist** for a full, booking-open class and receives **Waitlisted** only after the entry is committed. Before opting in, the member sees that this can automatically create an ordinary booking and that waiting gives no guaranteed seat. Failure — missing or invalid class reference, hidden class, unauthenticated or unauthorized caller, ineligible member, and cancelled, not-yet-open or closed occurrence yield the applicable unavailable, access, eligibility or closed state with no entry or booking. Boundary — if a seat is bookable and unowed to an eligible waiter when the join is decided, offer ordinary booking without automatically booking the member, including when an earlier full view is stale. Surface and confirmation — inspect the class action, the authoritative member/class state and occupancy before and after the join and each refusal (TC-01).
- **R-1.B One entry and no seat on join.** Positive — an accepted entry records its own admission order and survives reload. A repeated or concurrent join by the same member with an active entry returns that entry without changing its order. Failure — a member already booked sees **Booked**, with no added entry. A replay superseded by withdrawal, promotion or closure cannot create a new entry. Boundary — a member may be on waitlists for distinct occurrences if ordinarily eligible. Surface and confirmation — compare entry identity, order and booking count across two sessions and a different occurrence (TC-02, TC-04).
- **R-1.C Queue capacity.** Positive — with 19 active entries, the next eligible distinct member can occupy the twentieth place. Failure — with 20 active entries, a new distinct entrant sees **Waitlist full** and the queue is unchanged. Boundary — an existing member at the cap still sees **Waitlisted** and can leave. After withdrawal frees capacity, a fresh eligible join at 19 is admitted at the tail. Concurrent admissions cannot produce a twenty-first entry. Surface and confirmation — exercise the member action and compare ordered active identities at the booking boundary (TC-03).

**State and data.** Changes are limited to the queue for the requested occurrence and authenticated member. Initially the member has no entry, and admission creates one active entry keyed to that pair with an entry identity and a strict admission order. A successful join leaves confirmed occupancy unchanged. Duplicate joins preserve order, concurrent distinct admissions are ordered by authoritative admission, and no active entry is also a confirmed booking for the same pair. On an unknown result, refresh before retrying under R-5. An explicit fresh join and an earlier superseded join intent are distinct even if they target the same member and class. Checks cover the real admission interface and independent state assertions.

**Domain defaults and open gap.** The feature cap is 20 active entries per class, with one entry per member per occurrence and no separate cross-class quota. Class discovery owns visibility and the existing booking rules own eligibility and effective capacity. The product maintainer must locate those contracts and member/class identity semantics before wiring or checking live admission. In particular, verify any reservation holds that consume effective capacity and map their release to R-3 before using them in this model.

### R-2 Leave a waitlist without cancelling a booking

**Basis and delta.** Product source, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-21. Add withdrawal of the member's own pending enrollment, preserving ordinary booking cancellation and its rules. Member control is an inferred product default. No current cancellation surface was observed. Implementation and checks are pending.

- **R-2.A Withdrawal and rejoin.** Positive — choosing **Leave waitlist** removes the named active entry and enables a fresh join if R-1 permits it. Rejoining puts the member at the tail. Failure — an unauthorized caller cannot remove another member's entry. Boundary — a repeated leave for the removed entry is a no-op. A delayed leave for that entry does not remove a later entry, and an unknown write outcome is resolved by a read before a new intent. Surface and confirmation — use the class action and a subsequent authoritative read, comparing both entry identities and relative order (TC-04).
- **R-2.B Withdrawal racing with promotion.** Positive — withdrawal decided first excludes that member from promotion. Failure — promotion decided first returns **Booked** on the leave attempt, with the booking intact and ordinary cancellation available if permitted. Boundary — exact simultaneity has the same two outcomes according to authoritative decision order. Surface and confirmation — control both decision orders and assert the booking, seat count and remaining entry identities as well as the member status (TC-05).

**State and data.** The target is the member's specific entry instance for an occurrence. Before withdrawal it is active. After commit it is absent from the active queue and the other entries retain their relative order. Withdrawal cannot change a confirmed booking. A failed, uncommitted withdrawal leaves the entry eligible for promotion. Once committed, the entry is subject to R-4 removal.

**Domain defaults and open gap.** Rejoin has no priority carryover. The product maintainer must identify the existing cancellation entry point and behavior before the booked branch and race checks can be verified. There is no waitlist cancellation of a confirmed seat.

### R-3 Fill opened seats in admission order

**Basis and delta.** Product source, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-21. Infer that joining should lead to a real seat when one opens, and use automatic enrollment in first-in, first-out order. Preserve capacity and existing booking and cancellation rules. The seat-release paths and enrollment prerequisites are unobserved. Implementation and checks are pending.

- **R-3.A Allocate released seats.** Positive — when a still-open, full occurrence gains bookable capacity through a confirmed cancellation or another existing vacancy-producing operation, fill available seats with the earliest currently eligible entries in strict admission order. Each successful allocation creates one ordinary confirmed booking and removes that specific entry, including when neither member has the app open. Failure — if the allocation cannot be determined or committed, the attempted seat-changing operation does not commit a partial result and its caller receives a retryable failure. Boundary — with zero seats open allocate none. With one or several seats, allocate up to the number available. With an empty queue or after exhausting eligible entries, remaining seats follow ordinary booking. Surface and confirmation — use the real seat-release and booking boundaries with queued clients closed, then read bookings, queue order and occupancy (TC-05–08).
- **R-3.B Recheck before promoting.** Positive — an eligible head gets the next seat. Failure — a definitively ineligible entry is removed, and the next eligible entry can receive the seat. An eligibility lookup failure leaves eligibility unknown and triggers R-3.A failure handling. Boundary — at or after cutoff, or for a cancelled occurrence, no promotion occurs and R-4 ends the queue. Surface and confirmation — vary verified eligibility, unavailable eligibility, and authoritative time while observing committed booking identities and remaining entries (TC-06–08).
- **R-3.C Protect priority and capacity.** Positive — an ordinary booking competes for residual capacity after queued allocations. Failure — an ordinary booking attempt cannot claim a seat due to an eligible earlier waiter, and a replayed release of the same confirmed booking cannot create another vacancy. Boundary — concurrent releases, admissions, withdrawals and direct bookings resolve by authoritative decision order for the occurrence. Each successful decision leaves occupancy within effective capacity and at most one booking per member per class. Surface and confirmation — exercise both orders of direct-book/release, both leave/promotion orders, duplicate release and a two-seat release; compare confirmed identities and counts with the independently derived eligible prefix (TC-05–07).

**State and data.** A release targets a specific booking or authorized capacity change for one occurrence. The booking authority decides admission, release, allocation and ordinary booking against the same occupancy and queue rules. At observable commit, abandoned bookings release only their own seats, no eligible waiter is passed over for a walk-in booking, and booking and entry removal agree. One failed allocation leaves the release and queue in their prior state. After an interrupted response, the caller reconciles through authoritative readback. Existing authorized cancellation uses the existing outcome/recovery contract once verified. Older confirmed bookings count toward occupancy and are eligible sources of vacancies.

**Domain defaults and open gap.** The waitlist has no claim window or additional checkout step. Existing consent, payment, waivers, entitlement, scheduling conflicts and booking/cancellation limits still govern. The product maintainer must verify which actions free capacity, how the app handles waitlist consent and whether all enrollment effects and releases can meet the R-3 outcome contract, including any existing cancellation completion obligation. In particular, discover any mandatory interactive consent or payment and any release path that cannot participate in the same authoritative decision. These could conflict with the proposed automatic allocation or rollback rule. Dependent promotion and release integration are unready. If a conflict is found, record the evidence and resolve the affected product rule before integration.

### R-4 End the queue and keep waitlist information private

**Basis and delta.** Product source, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-21. Add the lifecycle and visibility rules for the new entries. Preserve existing identity, confirmed-booking retention and class cancellation policies. The runtime and data policy are unobserved. Implementation and checks are pending.

- **R-4.A End eligibility.** Positive — entries end on leave, promotion, definitive ineligibility found at allocation, class cancellation or cutoff. Failure — an ended entry cannot promote or be reactivated by a delayed request. Boundary — at the exact cutoff the queue is logically empty for reads and writes, including if class lifecycle cleanup has yet to run. A class reopened by ordinary policy starts with an empty waitlist after terminal closure. Surface and confirmation — read as each affected member and inspect authoritative active records across withdrawal, allocation, cancellation and times immediately before, at and after cutoff (TC-04, TC-06, TC-08).
- **R-4.B Minimize exposure.** Positive — return only the current member's own waitlist or booking status and applicable public action availability. The booking authority may read entry linkage and order to decide eligibility and allocation. Failure — reject unauthorized targeted reads and writes without disclosing another member's entry, identity, position or other booking details. Boundary — after logout, account switch or known revocation, clear affected private client state, ignore late responses for that identity and require current authority for further reads and writes. Surface and confirmation — inspect two member sessions, response payloads, authorization rechecks and the UI after identity changes (TC-09).
- **R-4.C Retention.** Positive — remove live entry data on leave, successful promotion or definitive removal, and remove closed or cancelled class entries through class lifecycle cleanup. Keep the existing booking record after promotion under the ordinary policy. Failure — a cleanup failure leaves terminal entries inaccessible and ineligible while the maintenance owner retries cleanup. Boundary — a fresh join after reopening or withdrawal gets new order and identity. Surface and confirmation — inspect class/entry state and closure cleanup, including controlled interruption and recovery (TC-04, TC-08).

**State and data.** An active entry survives authority restart until a terminal condition and needs only the existing member and class references, entry identity and ordering. Store it with the booking authority. The member sees their own status and actions. The client receives neither the private roster nor the ordering data. Client-only display state lasts for the current authorized view, and unfinished intents are discarded on identity change. No additional contact data, secret data, member-facing waitlist history or permanent entry tombstone is required. Confirmed bookings and existing backups stay under applicable retention and deletion policies. Waitlist diagnostic records follow the safe event scope in Service contracts.

**Domain defaults and open gap.** No public position, roster, staff reprioritization, or class-series waitlist. The product maintainer must verify the existing authorization/revocation, account and class deletion, rescheduling and diagnostics/purge policies, and identify the cleanup owner, physical deletion cadence and runtime limits before dependent data and compatibility work. Logical expiration is fixed above even if physical purge takes longer.

### R-5 Show the member a trustworthy result in the booking app

**Basis and delta.** Product source, “The incoming request,” 2026-11-21. Add waitlist status and actions to the class booking experience and show a promoted booking where ordinary confirmed booking appears. Preserve existing class and booking navigation and product identity. The current component and theme contracts are unobserved. Implementation and checks are pending.

- **R-5.A State and actions.** Positive — the class experience distinguishes bookable, full with **Join waitlist**, full with **Waitlist full**, **Waitlisted** with **Leave waitlist**, and **Booked** with the ordinary booking actions. An authoritative read after promotion shows **Booked** on the class and the ordinary confirmed booking surface. Failure — not-yet-open, closed/cancelled, ineligible and unauthorized states show the applicable condition with no unavailable join/leave controls. A class with no personal booking or waitlist entry shows a neutral state and whichever action its authoritative availability permits. Boundary — leaving, expiry and eligibility removal clear **Waitlisted** on the next successful read. Surface and confirmation — inspect both class and ordinary booking surfaces with the fixtures in TC-01, TC-03, TC-05 and TC-08.
- **R-5.B Pending, failure and recovery.** Positive — show loading while the initial status is unknown and an announced pending state on join or leave. Apply confirmed results only to the matching current class, member and interaction context. Failure — a definite rejection explains the applicable reason. An interrupted or timed-out write shows **Could not confirm. Refresh status** until authoritative readback, without inventing a success. If the read fails, keep recovery actionable. Boundary — stale responses, a reconnect or a screen closed during seat release cannot regress newer confirmed state. Reopening and explicit refresh use current state. Surface and confirmation — control response delivery in the UI and verify the readback, persistent recovery action, keyboard focus and announcements (TC-09–10).

**State and data.** The class component owns only pending/display state and an intent tied to its instance and currently authorized member. A mutation response means committed outcome or rejection. Loss of the response means unknown. Deduplicate repeated activation while pending and require readback before a fresh action on an unknown result. A newer authoritative view or changed interaction context supersedes an older response. Focus stays on the action or moves to the corresponding status or recovery control when the action disappears. Current confirmed booking survives reload through ordinary persistence.

**Domain defaults and open gap.** In-app status is the feedback channel. This feature adds no push, SMS or email. Preserve existing ordinary booking confirmation behavior once verified. The product maintainer must identify the actual booking/status surfaces and supported theme and accessibility primitives before component integration and consumer verification.

## Service contracts

The following specifies the logical boundary between the member app and current booking authority. It is an inferred integration boundary, with no transport, deployment or provider established.

- **Authority and freshness (R-1–R-5).** Own-entry read, join and leave use current authorization at the point of read or mutation. Seat decisions use current occupancy, eligibility and time. Cached display is provisional, cannot authorize a mutation or reveal state after known revocation, and is superseded by readback. Verify with stale availability, newly ineligible and revoked sessions (TC-01, TC-06, TC-09).
- **Incremental state (R-1–R-4).** Scope each mutation to the class, caller or authorized release and named intent. Preserve unrelated classes, members' bookings and the relative order of unaffected entries. Bound retries to their original intent so late replays cannot overwrite a newer lifecycle. Verify two occurrence identities and a stale withdrawal/replay (TC-02, TC-04).
- **Completion and recovery (R-2, R-3, R-5).** Member-initiated changes confirm only after commit. The seat-release path owns promotion independently of member clients. Before commit, failure leaves the prior authoritative state. After commit and response loss, readback returns the actual result. Verify failure on both sides of commit and all-or-nothing readback (TC-07, TC-10). A separate notification or asynchronous job boundary is inapplicable to the proposed contract. No shared cache, subscription or incremental-sync cursor is required.
- **Owned outcomes (R-3–R-5).** The booking authority supplies operational diagnostics for committed promotions, aborted allocations, authorization denials and cleanup failures, reusing current coverage where adequate. Include operation kind, outcome, safe correlation and only scope needed for diagnosis. Exclude raw member identifiers, contact details, credentials and payloads from added diagnostics. Existing authentication owns login coverage. Authorized operators verify retrieval and redaction for both outcomes of each affected path, including that an ordinary diagnostic sink failure preserves the business outcome and error. Current event coverage, safe retrieval, retention, and any mandatory audit failure policy are open. Verify existing coverage with isolated actions before extending it, and resolve conflicting audit rules before relying on this contract (TC-11).
