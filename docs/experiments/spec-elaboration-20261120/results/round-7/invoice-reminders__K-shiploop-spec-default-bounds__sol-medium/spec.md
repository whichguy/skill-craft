# Invoice Reminders

## Basis and scope

Source §Incoming request, 2026-11-21 — “Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.” The direct outcome is that a customer receives a reminder automatically while an invoice remains eligible and unpaid. The billing authority owns invoices, balances and recipients. An unattended reminder process evaluates them and hands off email to the mail boundary. The billing operator can verify processing through authorized operational evidence. The customer reads the reminder in their usual mail client.

This standalone stage has no prior specification, selected baseline, repository, discovery or research result. The requirements add a proposed reminder contract while preserving billing-owned data and payment workflows. Current invoice behavior and any existing automation are unknown. No external product supplies a parity reference. The schedule, channel and other product defaults are assumptions recorded in Submission.

## Requirements

### R-1 — Schedule automatic reminders

**Basis and delta.** Source §Incoming request, 2026-11-21, “Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.” Add unattended scheduling over billing-owned invoices. Invoice issue dates, due dates and payment terms remain billing-owned. Runtime scheduling, activation scope and existing automations are unobserved. Implementation and scheduler checks are pending.

**State and data.** Activation is scoped to the authorized billing account and recorded with an effective UTC instant by its activation owner. The scheduler reads it and operates independently of a client. Each evaluation combines the activation cutoff, current billing invoice and current time. Recovery recalculates from those authorities with R-3's saved slot disposition. Simultaneous evaluations obey R-3. No customer data is copied into scheduler configuration. Superseded activation configuration follows the billing environment's configuration lifecycle.

**Rules outside scope.** Custom cadence, local-time scheduling, holidays, manual sends and historical catch-up are excluded from the default. A skipped overdue invoice can qualify at a later in-window slot after activation.

**Open gap.** The billing owner must identify the target scope, authorized activation route and any existing reminder automation before activation. Confirm ownership of any overlapping schedule or establish a cutover that prevents duplicate sends. The platform owner must confirm an independent clock and scheduler and the recovery route before dependent runtime design. Calendar-rule definition can proceed independently.

**State and data.** The reminder runtime owns durable, account-scoped slot/attempt identity, disposition, count, timestamps, safe reason and any minimal reconciliation reference. A closed window permits no new work. A due-date correction can move the window for unstarted or definitively retryable work under R-1. Capture attempt identity before handing off email. Billing operators get only the operational evidence they are authorized to read. Customers see only their own reminder. Recipient address and body are transient within the reminder runtime; mail stores and recipient mailboxes follow their applicable policies. Existing approved retention applies to mail and diagnostics. Any permitted runtime secret is usable only by the authorized runtime and is excluded from reminder content and operational evidence.

**Rules outside scope.** Sender replies, bounce handling, tracking pixels, open/read tracking, manual retry, payment links and cross-channel fallback are excluded. A provider's own delivery events remain provider-owned.

**Open gap.** The delivery owner must establish an authorized sender and real test route, provider acceptance, rejection and timeout semantics, and a safe reconciliation or suppression path for ambiguous outcomes. The platform/billing owners must establish runtime-scoped durable state, ownership across restarts, approved retention and deletion, and operations retrieval before live work depends on them. ## Submission

**summary**

- Specified unattended overdue reminders delivered as plain-text email to the invoice's current billing recipient, with bounded cadence, current-data checks and per-slot recovery. The directory containing `GUIDES/` is the standalone working directory referred to as `KNOWLEDGE/`.
- Assumptions — Use UTC, three openings at days 1, 7 and 14 at 09:00, and a cutoff at day 21. This spaces reminders apart and gives the sequence a finite end. Skip openings before activation and elapsed windows to prevent a backlog burst. Use one ordinal per invoice for each reminder opportunity even when its due date changes. Use only the billing-designated recipient, invoice identifiers and balance, and defer while a payment is pending. These choices limit exposure and avoid sending while settlement is unresolved.
- Assumptions — Retry definitive transient non-acceptance at least one hour later, at most three attempts per slot. An uncertain submission takes the slot out of retry until authoritatively resolved. The last current billing/authorization decision controls an attempt, so a simultaneous payment or revocation can race with an already-authorized handoff. Use minimal account-scoped recovery state and inherited approved retention where applicable. No new product identity, settings or graphical UI is needed.
- Open prerequisites — Billing ownership, the status and recipient mapping, current scoped authority, stable invoice identity and existing automation/cutover are due before dependent live activation. Runtime facility, quotas, contention and recovery are due from the platform owner before dependent runtime planning. Sender authority, result semantics, mailbox proof, operational retrieval and retention/deletion checks are due from the delivery/platform owners before their affected live boundaries. Independent calendar, message and state-rule work can proceed. No conflicting product instruction was supplied. If target capabilities conflict with the required outcome, keep affected work unready and reconcile through the existing correction route; do not weaken acceptance to match implementation.
- Verification — Paper replays are recorded above. All product checks remain unverified. The delivery team owns controlled lower-layer checks at test and real boundary checks at system-test/product-acceptance, with release-only observations at release-verify. Person-only steps such as authorizing the billing scope or sender remain open with the respective billing or delivery owner at the phase whose effect they gate. A person-only mailbox confirmation without an authorized runner stays open with that owner at handoff unless it gates an earlier external effect. Product-acceptance may leave at most one primary requirement unverified and only with a passed lower-layer test for that requirement.
