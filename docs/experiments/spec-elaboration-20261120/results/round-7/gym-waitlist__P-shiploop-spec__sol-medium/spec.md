# Class Booking App

## Scope and sources

The [incoming request](#incoming-request-source) authorizes a waitlist for full class occurrences. An eligible member can queue, withdraw, and receive a booking when a seat becomes available. Treat each scheduled occurrence independently. Preserve compatible booking, cancellation, eligibility and class lifecycle behavior. The spec covers member-facing booking surfaces and the booking authority's corresponding changes. It adds no staff queue management, new identity, sharing, payment terms, outbound delivery channel or external service.

### Incoming request source

User, 2026-11-21, “The incoming request”  
> Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.

The standalone-stage instructions establish that there is no repository, prior specification, Discovery or Research result, or selected baseline. The request establishes that a class booking app and full classes are in scope. Existing behavior, surfaces and capabilities are unobserved. No reference product was supplied for functional parity. The preservation clauses below are obligations to verify against the actual app. The defaults are inferred product choices for this draft.

**State and data.** The booking authority owns the durable active-entry association between the existing member identity and the occurrence, including stable entry identity and order. It begins at committed join, carries no seat entitlement while active, and leaves the active queue on withdrawal, promotion, confirmed loss of eligibility, or class end of eligibility for booking. An entry is readable by its owner under current authorization and by the booking authority for allocation. Other members receive neither a roster nor peer identity or queue-position data. Before and after any join, at most one active entry or one active booking exists for the member–occurrence pair. A rejected join leaves both unchanged. R-4 governs uncertainty and retry.

**Rules left by the request and open gaps.** Default to the occurrence's existing booking eligibility and booking window. The product and booking owners must establish how membership, overlapping classes, booking caps, fees, credits and cancellation terms apply at join and promotion. In particular, establish that disclosed terms allow automatic booking and that any required consent can be captured by joining. Cases involving unknown policies and the live join binding are unready. The state model and eligibility-independent interaction design can proceed. Product owner and app owner confirm member routes, authorized fixture access and identity scope before planning their affected integration checks.

Any actual cross-service calls must be identified before integration, including their authority and failure behavior. ## Behavior model

### Journeys and transitions

These labels name product behavior. The booking authority means whichever existing part of the app owns admission and capacity. The member actions below are requests to that authority. Its confirmation means the change committed. Vacancy work may finish later, and subsequent reads show only committed booking state.

| Journey | Order and alternatives |
| --- | --- |
| F-1 Join and return (R-1, R-2, R-4) | Member opens an occurrence, reads availability and, if eligible and full, accepts the disclosed terms through **Join waitlist** (T-1). The action is pending until confirmed. After an interruption the member can obtain a current read (T-6). |
| F-2 Leave or reconcile a promotion (R-2, R-4) | Member with an active entry requests **Leave waitlist** (T-2). Leave winning ends that entry. Promotion winning leads the existing booking view to show the booking. A lost reply leads to a current read (T-6). |
| F-3 Release and refill (R-3, R-4) | Authorized actor cancels a confirmed booking (T-3). The booking authority resolves the vacancy with current eligibility (T-4). The promoted member reads back **Booked** (T-6). Closure (T-5) can supersede uncommitted allocation. An authorized new booker can take only an ordinarily bookable seat (T-7). |
| F-4 Close and observe (R-2, R-3, R-4) | Existing class lifecycle or booking cutoff ends allocation and active entries (T-5). Current authorized reads show the appropriate class state (T-6). |

| --- | --- | --- |

Design empty, loading, pending, success, unavailable and error states as part of that system. Use existing action, status and booking components when suitable. Confirm changes adjacent to the action, keep recovery available after errors, and announce async status accessibly without moving focus unnecessarily. Show visible keyboard focus, support keyboard and touch, and meet WCAG AA contrast for applicable text and controls. Motion only clarifies transitions and honors reduced motion. Inspect rendered states at the supported narrow and wide widths, in both themes, through keyboard, assistive status announcements, contrast measurement and reduced-motion checks. If the baseline leaves a new graphical treatment unspecified, use ShipLoop's light Google Gemini reference with Material 3 Expressive principles. Start from a light canvas with dark available for new web UI. Established identity, explicit constraints and platform appearance conventions take precedence. Set class and booking status in a clear typographic hierarchy, group state and action in a softly tinted surface, and reserve the strongest emphasis for the next action. The plan owns exact tokens and component treatment after the UI baseline is inspected. Compare the composed views against the baseline and direction at both widths. - **Integrity, recovery and authorization.** Under conflicting actions on one nearly full occurrence, simultaneous activity on different occurrences, a disconnection, and an interrupted allocation, verify the capacity, authorization, ordering and idempotency outcomes in R-1 through R-4. Verify through current member reads, authoritative booking outcomes, and approved diagnostic inspection. The diagnostic sink is not the source of completion. - **Workload, latency and runtime ceilings.** The observable baseline is that the first successful current read after a commit shows the committed outcome, and unresolved work is presented as such. Allocation completion and freshness budgets, concurrent demand on a popular class, cross-class throughput, queue and retention capacity, execution time, and cost limits are unresolved. The booking and operations owners must measure or obtain the existing workload, operating budgets, and hard platform limits before approving bounds and verifying representative contention, failure recovery and load. No numeric target or platform capability is established here.
- **Compatibility and operations.** Compare existing member routes, supported booking clients and authorized cancellation paths before and after the change. Verify they continue to apply their established policy and that the normal path still works for occurrences without active waiters. Platform, rollout, older-client compatibility and any active-entry migration or rollback constraints are open for the app and booking owners before affected release planning. A new uptime promise or external delivery SLA is outside the request because it introduces no new service or outbound channel.
