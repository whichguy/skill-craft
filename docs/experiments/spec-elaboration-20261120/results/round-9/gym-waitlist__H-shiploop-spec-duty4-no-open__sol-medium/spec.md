# Class Booking App

## Scope and basis

**Request, 2026-11-21 · The incoming request:** “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.”

A person who could otherwise book a full class can opt into that class occurrence's waitlist. An eligible person on the list gets a place when capacity becomes available, without needing to keep the app open or catch the vacancy. The person can check their own status and withdraw while waiting. The app's ordinary booking path, class capacity, eligibility, commercial terms and booking cancellation rules remain applicable.

The dated request establishes intent. This standalone stage has no earlier spec, discovery or research result, selected baseline, product repository, reference system or runtime. Existing product behavior is unknown. Statements about preserving booking rules describe the required relationship to the existing app, with discovery needed at the affected boundaries. Defaults below are product decisions for this spec. Verification examples use synthetic fixtures and establish logical expectations only.

The **booking authority** is the existing app authority for the specific occurrence, its capacity, the caller's identity and the right to book. **Open** means that authority still allows bookings for the occurrence. **Eligible** means the person meets its booking conditions other than seat availability and waitlist precedence. A **vacancy** is a place the authority currently makes available for booking. One participant's ordinary booking consumes one place in the acceptance fixtures. Real party-size or reserved-capacity rules must be recovered before testing the affected allocations.

## Requirements

### R-1 · Join the waitlist for a full class

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, *The incoming request*, “Add a waitlist to our class booking app.” Add opt-in on the existing booking surface for a full, open occurrence. Preserve the existing identity, eligibility and ordinary booking rules. The source establishes demand for a waitlist. The current entry point and the rules it enforces are unverified. Implementation and acceptance checks are pending.

- **Join.** Positive — an eligible caller viewing a full, open occurrence can choose **Join waitlist**, with clear notice that a place may be booked automatically under the applicable booking terms. The resulting confirmed state is **Waitlisted** for that occurrence. Failure — when the caller or occurrence is invalid, unauthorized, ineligible or closed, the attempt creates no membership or booking and gives the caller a permitted next step or reason. Boundary — fullness and eligibility are evaluated when the join is decided. If an ordinarily bookable place is now available after applying R-3, the stale join leaves the person unqueued and directs them to ordinary booking. Surface — class booking UI and authoritative membership and booking read-back. Confirm — compare full, available, closed and denied paths and read back both records in Cases A, D and E.
- **One active place in line.** Positive — an accepted join creates one membership for the caller and occurrence. Failure — a repeat or concurrent join for that caller and occurrence returns the existing outcome while membership remains active and keeps the original order. A delayed duplicate cannot recreate a membership the person already left or that the authority already fulfilled. Boundary — a person already booked for the occurrence cannot join. A fresh join after withdrawal is a new intent and takes a new place in line if the guards still hold. Surface — the caller's status and booking surface, plus authoritative checks of membership, order and booking. Confirm — submit duplicate joins and replay an old join after withdrawal, then make a fresh join as in Case A. Case B checks a fresh join by a participant who is already booked.

**State and data.** The booking authority owns membership scoped to the existing participant identity and class occurrence, with an identity for each membership and an authoritative order. Before a successful join there is no active membership or confirmed booking for that pair. After it there is one active membership and no confirmed booking. An unsuccessful join leaves both resources as they were. A lost acknowledgement is resolved under R-5.

**Domain bounds and open prerequisites.** Waitlisting concerns one occurrence, with the same visibility and booking eligibility as that occurrence. There is no feature-specific numerical list cap. Booking maintainer, due in discovery before integration design — inspect the current full-class and available-class flows, the participant and occurrence identifiers, and the actual eligibility and consent rules. These gate integration and real-surface verification. The synthetic join rules remain checkable independently.

### R-3 · Fill a vacancy from the waitlist

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, *The incoming request*, “so full classes don't turn people away.” Add vacancy-to-booking progression for waitlisted participants. Preserve capacity, current booking eligibility, current terms and the ordinary confirmation. Cancellation, inventory, booking side effects and recovery were unavailable for inspection. Implementation and acceptance checks are pending.

- **Allocate in order.** Positive — by default, an open occurrence's vacancies go in order of accepted join, earliest eligible member first, until the available places or eligible members run out. Selection uses the booking authority's order when joins overlap. Permanent or temporary known ineligibility for this allocation skips the member, whose membership remains active and retains its order while the class is open. Failure — when eligibility or completion cannot be determined, preserve the unresolved allocation and prevent a later member or direct booker from taking the affected place until reconciliation. A blocked direct booking gives the caller retry feedback. Boundary — zero vacancies yield zero promotions. Once eligible waiting demand is exhausted, remaining available places follow ordinary booking. Surface — ordinary booking status for each participant and the authoritative ordered membership and capacity view. Confirm — replay vacancy, empty and ineligible-list outcomes and the competing direct booking in Cases B, C and F.
- **Complete a real booking.** Positive — by default the opt-in authorizes automatic booking under the existing terms. Promotion succeeds when one ordinary confirmed booking and the end of that person's active membership are durably established together as one consumer-visible outcome. It proceeds with the waiting client closed. Failure — a failed or interrupted promotion cannot silently lose either the person's turn or the capacity, and recovery must converge to one booking with no active membership or a recoverable active membership with no booking. Boundary — one remaining place yields at most one booking, also across simultaneous joins, cancels, withdrawals, direct bookings or repeated vacancy processing. Surface — the ordinary booking view, class availability and the authoritative record boundary. Confirm — inspect capacity and the person's two records after controlled competing operations and failures before and after commitment as in Cases B and C.

**State and data.** A cancellation or other supported capacity change alters the affected occurrence's inventory. Promotion may change its booking and one membership from active/unbooked to ended/booked. At every externally confirmed outcome, occupancy stays within allocatable capacity and the promoted participant is booked only once. Fulfillment runs under the booking authority and rechecks participant eligibility, membership and class openness at the decision. A definitive eligibility rejection leaves the membership active and lets the authority consider other members for that vacancy. A rejected or interrupted write for an otherwise eligible member triggers recovery before moving past that member. Reconcile on a vacancy while open, on any direct booking attempt with available capacity, and when an eligibility change takes effect for a waitlisted member with a vacancy still open. An indeterminate effect reserves its logical place in the allocation order until reconciled. Retry or recovery operates on the same occurrence and outcome, including after restart, and revalidates current state. A confirmed closure takes precedence over any still-uncommitted promotion under R-4.

**Domain bounds and open prerequisites.** The booking maintainer must recover, before allocation integration, the real vacancy and eligibility-change producers, effective capacity, booking guards, authoritative order and write scope, existing side effects and unattended recovery path. Confirm the normal terms can be honored from this opt-in. Earlier unresolved allocations cannot be bypassed. Staff priority, new fees or entitlements, offer-and-claim windows, and new delivery channels are outside scope. Existing confirmation effects, if applicable, follow the recovered booking contract.

### R-4 · Stop waiting when booking closes

**Basis and delta.** The 2026-11-21 request, *The incoming request*, “waitlist” for classes implies a lifetime bounded by the opportunity to book. - **Close the list.** Positive — when the authority closes bookings or cancels the occurrence, the waitlist ends and the accessible occurrence shows its closed or cancelled state. Failure — stale joins and still-uncommitted promotions produce no new memberships or bookings after closure. Boundary — the existing booking cutoff applies, with class start as the default only if the app has no cutoff. At the effective cutoff and later, the list is closed to joins and promotions. - **Remove ended membership.** Positive — ended membership immediately loses active participation and leaves the active user view. Failure — a failed purge leaves membership inactive and is subject to the existing accountable recovery process. Boundary — active membership data ends on withdrawal, fulfillment, closure or applicable removal of the participant or occurrence. Default to retaining no separate user-visible waitlist history. Surface — authorized status reads and operator-authorized state inspection. Confirm — inspect state after closure or withdrawal, and verify deletion and recovery using the actual retention facilities.

**State and data.** The authority owns occurrence availability and membership lifecycle. Closing a class ends active membership for that occurrence and bars pending work from creating new bookings. Closure and allocation decide against consistent occurrence state. The client shows the ordinary unavailable state if the occurrence is inaccessible. Records kept only for existing audit, deletion recovery or protecting against delayed operations follow the applicable policy and may never restore active membership.

**Domain bounds and open prerequisites.** Booking maintainer and data owner, due in discovery before lifecycle and purge integration — establish real cutoff and closure events, reopen semantics, authorized deletion events and enforceable retention, including replicas, audit records and backups where applicable. If later carried through product acceptance, each unobserved outcome must have its reason, settling observation, owner and due phase recorded in `unverified`. A primary outcome also needs a passed lower-layer test for that requirement, otherwise use add-work. If later discovery invalidates a premise, use the existing correction/revision route, preserve the original baseline, update the affected clauses and replay stale checks.
