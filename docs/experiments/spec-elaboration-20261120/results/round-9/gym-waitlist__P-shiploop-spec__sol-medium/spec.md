# Class Booking App

## Scope and source

The user request dated 2026-11-21, under **The incoming request**, says “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” **The stage**, duties 2–10 and 12, supplies the specification and quality obligations. A member who cannot book a full class can join its waitlist, see their own standing, and receive a confirmed booking if an eligible seat becomes available. They can withdraw while waiting.

The request establishes that the product books classes and that classes can be full. Existing booking, cancellation, eligibility, capacity, identity, and UI contracts are preservation obligations. Their implementation and exact rules were unavailable: this standalone run has no repository, maintained spec, discovery or research output, selected baseline, or reference product. The behavior below defines the proposed change. It supplies no observation of the existing app and claims no parity, runtime capability, or test pass.

Use the exact scheduled occurrence as the class identity and the existing authenticated booking principal as the member identity. “Available seat” means an unallocated seat within the authority's current capacity and booking rules. “Booking open” means the existing booking authority currently permits allocation for that occurrence. Start and cancellation are terminal for the waitlist. A temporary booking restriction does not end the waitlist.

## Requirements

Each acceptance row names the expected positive, failure, and boundary observations and the surface and method for confirming them. Case references point to independent walkthroughs or planned observations below. Implementation and candidate checks for every requirement are pending.

### R-1 — Join a full class's waitlist

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, **The incoming request**, “Add a waitlist to our class booking app so full classes don't turn people away.” Preserve direct booking where a seat is available, existing eligibility and access checks, and occurrence identity. Add an explicit opt-in on an eligible full occurrence. No baseline was available to verify the preserved behavior or select an integration point.

**Rules and state.** The authenticated member requests to join for themselves and sees before confirming that gaining a seat automatically creates a booking under the applicable booking and cancellation rules. The booking authority checks current access, eligibility, occurrence state, and capacity. A successful join creates one active entry for the member and occurrence with a stable order assigned when the join commits. A member cannot hold both a confirmed booking and an active entry for the same occurrence. A retry of the same intent resolves the original outcome and returns current authorized status, even after the entry ends. When the original outcome can no longer be recovered under the applicable retention policy, require read-back and an explicit new action before creating any entry. A new intent while already waiting returns their current entry without changing order. After withdrawal, an explicit fresh join qualifies under the same rules and goes to the back. A seat opening between display and join yields updated availability and the ordinary booking action if available after prior waiters are served. The join action itself cannot directly book a seat.

### R-2 — Allocate released seats to eligible waiters

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, **The incoming request**, “so full classes don't turn people away.” Preserve existing legitimate bookings, cancellation terms, and eligibility. Introduce priority for waiters when a seat becomes allocatable. First committed join first, with direct creation of a booking after opt-in, is the unattended default. It gives the member a route into class without an expiring claim window. There is no baseline for release paths, booking side effects, or worker capability.

**Rules and state.** For each exact occurrence, the booking authority gives the earliest committed, currently eligible active entry first access to each released seat. It defines a total commit order for simultaneous joins. On a definite eligibility failure, end that member's entry as ineligible, expose the reason as permitted, and consider the next entry. If eligibility is indeterminate or processing fails, keep the affected valid position and protect openings from direct allocation until recovery. Changes that may create an allocatable seat, including a valid cancellation, a permitted capacity increase, or reopening with spare capacity, trigger reconciliation. Openings with no eligible active entry use ordinary direct booking after any definitive disqualifications have been resolved. An ordinary booking request cannot take a seat owed to an earlier eligible waiter while reconciliation is pending.

A promotion consumes one seat, creates one confirmed booking for the waiter, and ends that active entry as promoted as one observable decision. A partial result cannot present both an active entry and a confirmed booking or report success prematurely. Concurrent allocations, withdrawals, starts, and class cancellations resolve at the booking authority against current state. Reconciliation runs with the member's screen closed. Repeated or late reconciliation rechecks current occurrence state, remaining capacity, and the current entry. Pending processing is recoverable after interruption, and a committed promotion is discoverable after an acknowledgment is lost. A cancelled or started occurrence cannot receive a new promotion. If a promotion precedes an occurrence cancellation, the confirmed booking follows the existing class-cancellation rules.

If eligibility is unknown, leave the affected entry waiting and protect the opening. | One opening in a two-member queue promotes only the eligible head. Simultaneous openings and repeated or overlapping processing never exceed capacity or create duplicate bookings. | Authoritative booking and waitlist read-backs plus each member's booking and status surfaces. **Open gaps and omitted domain rules.** Booking owner, due before planning release integration: inventory all supported capacity and booking writers, the existing class-change rules, time authority and eligibility checks. The default has one queue, no reserved categories, and no external notification. A member sees promotion in their own booking and waitlist surfaces under R-4.

### R-3 — Withdraw and end waiting

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, **The incoming request**, “Add a waitlist,” with withdrawal and automatic expiry as lifecycle defaults. Preserve existing member booking cancellation and occurrence cancellation behavior. Add self-withdrawal and automatic waitlist termination. Existing cancel permissions, history, and expiry surfaces were unavailable.

**Rules and state.** A member can withdraw their own active entry, even during a temporary booking restriction, subject to current authority to manage that entry. Withdrawal applies to the exact entry, even if a future join is for the same occurrence. An already-withdrawn entry gives an idempotent result. A stale withdrawal after promotion reports that the member has a booking and offers the ordinary booking controls where permitted. It has no booking-cancellation effect. If withdrawal commits first, a later seat allocation skips that entry. Cancellation of the class or arrival at its scheduled start ends every remaining active entry and makes them ineligible for allocation immediately. A delayed processing attempt must enforce that terminal guard independently of whether cleanup has run.

### R-4 — Show the member's own waitlist and booking status

**Basis and delta.** 2026-11-21 request, **The incoming request**, “Add a waitlist to our class booking app,” with duty 9 of **The stage** for state, information, and feedback. Preserve the existing app's class and booking journeys, identity, and appearance conventions. Add waitlist actions and status to the affected class and member booking surfaces. The actual UI, account model and existing status refresh capabilities were unavailable.

**Rules and state.** The class surface presents the current applicable action for the signed-in member: book, join with the auto-booking explanation, leave while waiting, or view their confirmed booking. The member booking surface shows their active entries with occurrence details and their own current ordinal among active entries in committed order. As entries ahead leave or end, a new authoritative read updates the ordinal. It is a place in the queue at the time of the read. A confirmed promotion appears as an ordinary booking and resolves the waiting status. For a visible ended entry, show the applicable outcome, such as withdrawal, closure, or eligibility loss, with no active position. If booking and joining are unavailable, show the relevant status and omit unavailable actions. If an opening is being reconciled, clarify that immediate booking is unavailable while it is allocated. An empty waitlist shows an intentional empty state. Re-entering or refreshing the relevant surface revalidates current state and authority. The UI shows pending work, unknown outcome with recovery, and confirmed outcomes distinctly. Superseded or prior-account results cannot update the current view.

## State, interaction and service contracts

The booking authority is the logical owner already required by a shared class booking product. The product's actual runtime boundaries and placement are open. The roles below describe observable responsibilities. No separate service or client-side durable queue is required by this spec.

| State and requirements | Ownership, access and lifecycle |

| Occurrence and booking, R-1 to R-3 | Existing booking authority owns the occurrence, capacity, class lifecycle and confirmed bookings. The member may read their own permitted booking and class details. Authorized operations use the existing scopes. Promotion has the same confirmed-seat invariant and downstream obligations as an ordinary booking. Booking records follow established retention and removal. |
| Waitlist entry and position, R-1 to R-4 | The booking authority owns minimal occurrence and member references, committed order, entry state and the promotion link where applicable. Active waiting survives client closure and is unique per member and occurrence. Terminal outcomes are withdrawn, promoted, ineligible and ended by cancellation or start. | Intent and recovery, R-1 to R-3 | The authority owns enough state to identify a retry, bind it to the original principal, occurrence and action or entry, and distinguish an unknown result from a new intent. Remove recovery or retry material once its obligation ends under the established retention policy. |
| Presentation, R-1, R-3 and R-4 | The member's view holds transient loading, pending, error and confirmed display state for the current account and occurrence. **Member app ↔ booking authority.** Use current booking identity/access for every protected read and write. Displayed availability can age, but never authorizes a mutation. After any successful mutation, the next authorized read reflects the committed result. A later account or authorization change is enforced independently of data age. Incremental updates affect the identified occurrence and member entry or booking only, with unrelated records preserved. Completion means durable join/withdrawal or confirmed booking, as applicable. If the connection is interrupted, read back under current authority and resolve the same intent. Verify against the real app boundary with account switching, delayed responses, before/after records for an unrelated occurrence, and repeat requests (Cases 2 and 3).

**Capacity change ↔ allocation responsibility.** The authority accepts actual changes through the supported booking/capacity operations. Recovery tracks unresolved effects of committed releases and rechecks current capacity, membership, eligibility and terminal state before allocation. Duplicate or out-of-order processing does not overwrite a newer decision. A release acknowledgment confirms the release, while promotion is confirmed by its booking and ended-entry read-back. The booking runtime owns recovery with the screen closed. Verify with interrupted processing, concurrent release and booking, and reconciliation after restart (Cases 4–6). A separate worker, event channel or external authority is unconfirmed. The platform owner must map any actual boundary and prove its permissions, recovery and capacity guarantees before dependent implementation.

**Owned event coverage.** The booking authority is the source for join/withdrawal outcomes, promotion/eligibility outcomes, authorization denials and release reconciliation failures/recovery. Reuse adequate existing approved logging and access-controlled retrieval. Extend coverage only for missing owned outcomes. Preserve existing identity-provider login coverage and avoid duplicating it. Authorized operators need safe correlation sufficient to follow release and recovery. Diagnostic logging failure cannot masquerade as a committed booking or reverse a committed decision. Omit names, contact details, account secrets, raw payloads and peer queue records from waitlist diagnostics. The booking and privacy owners must verify the actual event sink, authorized retrieval, redaction and retention policy before observability integration, and demonstrate an interrupted promotion and recovered outcome at system-test. No new external notification delivery applies because the default uses app read-back.

**Runtime mapping and quotas.** Logical needs are current access enforcement, authoritative shared state, coordinated allocation, durable recovery without a browser, and time/closure enforcement. Runtime, quota and retention evidence is unavailable. Before dependent plans, the platform owner must map each need to actual guarantees and limits for persistence, execution/recovery, contention, and request volume. Establish representative independent-class and hot-class loads with the product owner. Validate that recovery and retries stay within selected platform quotas and that quota or transient failures leave a recoverable state. No platform-specific limit or runtime guarantee is asserted here.

## Submission

**summary** — The spec defines joining a full class's waitlist, receiving a released seat, withdrawing, and seeing one's own status. Assumptions are first committed join first, explicit opt-in to automatic booking, in-app confirmation, withdrawal until promotion, termination at start or class cancellation, and skipping a definitively ineligible entry. Own position is shown on read-back and a new join after withdrawal goes to the end of the queue. No material conflict is established by the available sources. Existing booking consent, payment, eligibility, lifecycle and authority rules could conflict with a default. They remain open under the affected requirements and gate only dependent integration and verification. Runtime, supported UI, quotas, retention and fixture capabilities also remain open. The logical behavior and independent case design can proceed. The six paper walkthroughs were checked against the specification. Product verification is pending.
