# Invoicing

## Scope and basis

The request dated 2026-11-21, under “The incoming request,” is “Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices.” Its direct outcomes are background execution at policy-defined times (R-1), restricting reminders to eligible unpaid invoices (R-2), and delivering an invoice-specific reminder to the authorized recipient (R-3). R-4 supports those outcomes through recovery and truthful reporting.

The standalone stage supplies no prior specification, discovery, research, selected baseline, repository, reference system, or runtime. The request is the source for intended outcomes. The criteria below derive the minimum behavior needed to meet them, with unknown business rules and capabilities marked open. There is no observed before-state or parity claim. The feature uses the billing authority's records and leaves invoice and payment mutation with their existing owners. Creating invoices, collecting payments, adding a settings interface, and adding manual reminders are outside this request.

Product rules block calendar and catch-up behavior and their checks. An implementation owner must identify the supported unattended execution route and runtime identity before runtime integration. Due-instant comparison, session independence and the eligibility guard can be specified and checked independently with isolated fixtures.

**State and lifecycle.** The reminder processing authority owns occurrence outcome and correlation within billing scope. Its recovery-relevant information must survive interruption of processing. The runtime keeps only the identifying references and facts needed to recover or explain the outcome, with read access limited to scoped operations and runtime roles. A due occurrence may be held or suppressed, begin processing, be confirmed accepted or rejected, or remain unresolved after an interrupted handoff. The current recovery authority may reconcile an unresolved outcome. Concurrent workers and delayed receipts cannot reopen a confirmed handoff or overwrite a newer authoritative outcome. Diagnostic evidence is distinct from the state needed to prevent repeats. Remove correlation and outcome data under the approved retention policy once recovery, repeat protection and applicable obligations permit it. Expiry cannot make a still-replayable confirmed occurrence send again.

**Open gaps.** The integration owner must confirm recovery state and processor ownership, the supported protection against concurrent submissions, transport correlation/read-back and duplicate guarantees, storage and diagnostic custody, retention/purge and runtime quotas. The billing policy owner must settle a retry and expiry policy where retry is permitted, including the treatment of unknown outcomes that cannot be reconciled. Fixed delays, attempt counts and retention periods have no grounded default. Until safe recovery is established, unknown outcomes remain inspectable and ineligible for blind repeat. These gaps block the affected recovery route and capacity checks. Specification and fixture verification of truthful outcomes and safe suppression can proceed independently.

### summary

Specified unattended timing, current unpaid eligibility, recipient delivery and safe recovery. The spec distinguishes modeled expectations from target evidence. No production bound value was chosen as a default. The request supplies no timing policy, reference system, operating limits or delivery policy from which to derive a reversible choice. The alternatives and required evidence are recorded under the affected requirements.

Open for the billing policy owner in R-1 through R-4 are timing, eligibility details, recipient/channel policy and permitted recovery windows. Open for the integration owner are the target, authority and freshness, payment/dispatch ordering, delivery and reconciliation capabilities, state lifetime and runtime quotas. These block only their dependent criteria and runtime work. The independently defined eligibility, dispatch and recovery invariants can proceed through fixture-based specification and planning. No material conflict between supplied requirements was identified. Payment/handoff ordering and recovery versus duplicate prevention remain open integration concerns. Implementation and product verification remain pending.
