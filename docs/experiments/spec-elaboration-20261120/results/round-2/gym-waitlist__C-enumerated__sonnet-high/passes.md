## Pass 2: what each sentence takes for granted

Added C-36 (seat size), C-37 and C-38 (every way a seat opens, the five events enumerated), C-39 (time bound), C-40 (message failure never undoes a seat), C-41/C-42 (who may act, who may hold an entry), C-43 (a member's own state on the class page), C-44 (entries reserve nothing).
"so full classes don't turn people away": assumes a definition of full (C-2, already), that turning away today is a refusal, and that "people" are members. Covered by C-2, C-42.
Own clauses re-asked: "booked automatically" needs an order (C-6), eligibility (C-12), a cost rule (C-11), a trigger list (C-37). "Notified" needs a channel and an answer for members who cannot be reached (C-32, C-40). "Limit" needs a number (C-35). "Refused" needs a stated reason (U-5 list, C-19 to C-26). Found nothing further on the member side.
