# Passes

## Pass 1 — the ground
Nothing exists in the working directory (empty repo, no code, no prior spec), so everything is stated rather than inherited. Identified the people: account Owner/Admin, view-only Member, invoice recipient (customer), operator. Wrote U-1..U-15 covering ordinary use, first time, existing data, unhappy paths (invalid config, settled-in-the-moment, bounces), and operator work. Wrote the core clause for each (C-1..C-22). Nothing found on: billing/legal aspects beyond sender identity (deferred to pass 4).

## Pass 2 — what each sentence takes for granted
Request sentence "Set up automatic reminders for unpaid invoices" taken apart: "set up" (who may, what is configured, what is required first, bounds on each field), "automatic" (what triggers, what time, which time zone, DST), "reminders" (channel, content, language, recipient, one or many per invoice), "unpaid invoices" (which states, what settled means, partial payment, no due date, no address). Added C-23..C-41. Then put each new clause through the same question (e.g. the schedule takes for granted offset range, uniqueness, an empty schedule, a stored time zone; the amount takes for granted currency and decimals; the roles take for granted who can only view). Added C-96, C-97 from that recursion.

## Pass 3 — where the parts meet
Parts: schedule, invoice state, due date, payments, pause, customer stop link, manual send, test send, retries, suppression list, operator switch, account switch, scheduler runs, provider feedback. Pairs walked: due-date change vs sent history (cycles, C-42/43), schedule edit vs invoices in flight (C-44/45/63), account turned on vs overdue invoices (C-44), payment vs send in the same moment (C-48), two runs at once (C-49), crash between send and record (C-50), manual vs automatic (C-51/52), pause vs edits (C-55/56), stop vs resume vs settled (C-57/58), operator switch vs missed steps (C-61), late bounces (C-69), multiple due steps (C-46/47). Added C-42..C-72 plus C-98 later.

## Pass 4 — the runtime's own rules
Runtime: an email provider plus a scheduled job on a hosted platform. Added: provider rate cap and large batches (C-73, figure unconfirmed), sender authentication SPF/DKIM/DMARC and domain verification before production (C-74, C-75), From/Reply-To (C-76), bounce/complaint feedback delay (C-77), scheduler cadence and alert (C-78), daylight-saving gap and repeat (C-79, C-80), job time limit/interruption (C-81, figure unconfirmed), attachments/spam filtering (C-82), non-production allow-list (C-83), time display (C-84). Legal note: reminders are treated as transactional billing mail; accurate sender identity and a contact route are required (C-10, C-76); a postal-address requirement is deliberately left out because it applies to commercial mail, and the request describes billing mail.

## Pass 5 — what must not change, what must never happen
Added retention and immutability (C-85, C-86), invoices untouched by reminders (C-87), identity never crossing accounts (C-88), no leakage (C-89), same rules on every sending path (C-90), existing invoice functions unchanged and rollback by switching off (C-91), pre-existing data (C-92), operator observation and per-account disable (C-93, C-94), failed-reminders list (C-95).

## Pass 6 — grind (first read-through as the builder)
Found and fixed: C-20 was two behaviours (split into C-20, C-38, C-39); C-12, C-18, C-19 each carried a second behaviour (trimmed, the behaviour moved to C-89, C-85, C-84 or C-48/C-57); C-62 was two behaviours (split into C-65 and C-67); C-22 had no precedence among states (order added); manual send did not refuse Draft invoices (added); C-17 did not say which failures are transient (added); the operator-disabled account was not in the pre-send checks (added); the list of what Members can view omitted the suppression list (added); the sizes of billing address lists were unbounded (C-98). Also made C-38/43/44 distinguish events that start a cycle from events that only skip steps dated up to the event day.

## Pass 7 — grind (final)
Re-read the whole specification looking for: an Observable that cannot be seen (none left; each names a screen, email, header, list or history row); a word two readers would read differently ("settled" is defined in C-9, "cycle" in C-43, "send moment" in C-7, "day" in C-44, "unsettled" means not settled); a number nobody wrote (every limit now has one: 10 steps, -60..+365, 3 retries, 15 minutes, 3-day window, 24 hours, 1,000, 5 addresses, 100/2,000 characters, 10 tests/hour, 128 bits, 60 minutes, 90 days, 24 months); a use case with no unhappy path (U-1..U-15 each map to a refusal, skip or failure clause); a piece of state with no end (pause ends on resume, suppression on clear, recipient stop is permanent by C-57, test counters roll hourly, history ends at 24 months); a silent decision (every default is a Source: choice); a clause that is really two (none found). This pass added nothing a builder would otherwise have got wrong; it is the stopping pass.

Items deliberately left out and why: editable templates (C-33), per-customer schedules (C-24), SMS or other channels (C-23), business-day shifting (C-5), grouping several invoices into one email (C-36), a postal address in the footer (pass 4), notification emails to the business for each reminder (the history and failed list serve), customer-side unsubscribe across all invoices (the stop link is per invoice).
