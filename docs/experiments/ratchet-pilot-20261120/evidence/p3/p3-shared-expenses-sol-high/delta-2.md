# Closing look

Reviewed the original request, the existing specification and delta, both cycles of build and test reports, `fix-1.md`, `plan.md`, the README, and the service and test sources. The workspace had no referee report. All 24 tests passed with `node --test`.

Ran the current server and a temporary copy of the cycle 1 build on OS-assigned ports with temporary stores. Replayed the README's equal and exact expenses and checked balances, settlements, duplicate creation, a one-cent expense, a fractional amount and an unknown group with malformed JSON. Exercised the literal dot and question-mark IDs, the ASCII and emoji ID boundaries, short and oversized queries, the expanded amount boundary, and an exact share larger than its total. Restarted after both normal shutdown and a forced stop. Also exercised a store produced by the earlier build, a directory that became available after startup, final symbolic links, hard links, and external restoration by rename and by timestamp-preserving copy.

## Additions

- **C-97 — Stale writes after the configured store changes**. product keeps to it: no. Replacement by rename gave 500 and preserved the replacement. With `cp -p` into the existing file, the different snapshot retained the same inode, length and modification time. The expense returned 201 with `{"expense":2}`. Restart recovered Ava's 5-cent balance from the stale snapshot plus the new expense. Applying the expense to the restored snapshot would have yielded 3 cents.
- **C-98 — Compatibility across admission limits**. product keeps to it: yes. The cycle 1 build acknowledged a 49,153-byte group ID and a separate 100,001-digit expense. The current service loaded their snapshot, served all three group routes for the long ID, reported the older expense exactly in balances and settlements, and continued numbering across another restart.
- **C-99 — Documented per-expense capacity and larger derived results**. product keeps to it: yes. Two `9e99999`-cent expenses succeeded. The 100,001-digit total appeared exactly as an unquoted JSON number in balances and settlements, including after a forced stop and restart. A new `1e100000`-cent expense received 500, and a share of that value against a one-cent total received 400.
- **C-100 — Separate HTTP size budget**. product keeps to it: yes. The README states the separate shared budget for the target and headers. A maximum-length ASCII ID returned identical balances with no query and a 16,000-byte query suffix. A 16,384-byte suffix returned 431 on GET and POST, with the persisted bytes unchanged.
- **C-101 — Hard-linked store paths**. product keeps to it: yes. Starting with either hard-linked name returned a startup error and preserved both paths' original bytes. Starting through the original path worked after removing the extra link.
- **C-102 — JSON response media type**. product keeps to it: yes. The expense, balances and settlements responses each carried `Content-Type: application/json`.
- **U-21 — Handover after late store availability**. product keeps to it: yes. The early process refused a write while another process owned the new store. After that owner shut down, the early process's first balances GET recovered the saved expense. Its next expense was numbered 2, and a restart recovered both contributions.
