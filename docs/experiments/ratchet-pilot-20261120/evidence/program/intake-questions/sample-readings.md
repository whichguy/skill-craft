specification (`work/docs/shiploop/spec.md`, or `current-spec.md`), the other knowledge files in the same
folder (`environment.md`, `architecture.md`) and the discovery stage's result file. "Answered" means a line there
states the fact the question asks for. "census flags" are the script's reference tests for the same question

for the asked fact (#14). Of the 14, 3 are answered only in `environment.md` / `architecture.md` and not in
`spec.md` (#5, #7, #11). In 13 of 15 the discovery stage's result also states the answer for that question (#6 only
has a general "Q1-Q10 resolved by ..." line, and #14 only partly). The census id test flagged 10 of the 15 (id),
and 3 of the 15 (id+words: #7, #9, #15), although 14 of 15 have the answer on record: the answer often sits in
the knowledge files or the discovery text without the id `Qn` being cited near the question's own words.

Caveat on the sample: questions in the older ledgers (2026-10-31 to 2026-11-04) are generic one-line wordings
("What existing behavior exists?"), and many of the answers are "the repository is empty", which a reader can
settle from the checkout in seconds; the Sonnet 2026-11-21 ledgers are more specific. I did not check each
stated answer against the code; I only checked that an answer is stated.
