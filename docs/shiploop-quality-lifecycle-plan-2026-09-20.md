## Outcome and boundaries

Every affected verifiable clause retains its required verification surface,
case or other existing verification record and due phase through actual observation. Local tests do
not discharge a requested deployed interaction. Improve receives the actual
candidate scope, including selected untracked work. Current workspace-return
status is derived from its verified receipt rather than copied historical prose.

Definition of Ready means the current activity has the inputs and prerequisites
it needs. Definition of Done means that activity produced its promised artifact
and evidence. A completed definition, test plan or authored test is not a passed
product test. Pre-release product acceptance may retain post-release checks as
pending; final delivery cannot claim them passed before their observations exist.

Keep existing Markdown records and result/evidence fields authoritative. Do not
add another graph, acceptance state machine, test catalog, mandatory commit,
review hash gate, or universal custom app/chrome requirement. The delivery
contract remains an explicitly selected new-run pilot. Review tiering remains a
separate experiment; unchanged files alone do not justify skipping review.

Improve remains the existing child after each producer. It reviews that stage's
artifact, edit bounds and expected check state. A planning or expected-RED review
cannot demand future product success. A later-stage review must inspect actual
evidence rather than merely accepting a planned test or repeated conclusion.
